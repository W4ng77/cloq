- Local defects and final failure do not correspond one-to-one; some successful continuations contain later corrections.
- Within rescue and damage states, the failing continuation carries the `unintended_or_unconsented_change` label more often than the succeeding continuation of the same state (paired rows above). This describes how failing branches look given outcome-defined groups; it is not a cause of the sign.
- Mechanically, whether a write executes does not separate the branches (rescue QQ vs BQ 17 vs 17; damage 15 vs 14). Executed writes without recorded operation-specific consent: rescue 9 vs 5 (diff +0.20, Newcombe [-0.06, +0.43]), damage 3 vs 3. In damage states the BQ `unintended_or_unconsented_change` labels are mostly on writes WITH recorded consent (7/20 states), i.e. the semantic 'unintended' part (consented but wrong change), which overlaps with the DB-based outcome by construction (rescue QQ: 2/20).
