### 3.4 New direction inspired by arXiv 2606.00206 (running; W88)

- **What that paper does:** "Quantized Reasoning Models Think They Need to Think Longer" (NeurIPS 2026 oral) explains quantization damage with a token-level mechanism: high-KL positions have high entropy, and there the quantized model over-samples markers like "wait"/"but". A training-free logit penalty both fixes the damage and validates the mechanism. It covers 1.5–32B models.
- **Our analogue, running now:**
  1. At every Bank U state, teacher-force a_B and a_Q under both models (top-20 prompt logprobs). Measure entropy, KL and the margin at the first divergent token, compare flip vs no flip and rescue vs damage, and classify the divergent token (confirmation phrase, question, discourse marker, tool/arg, number/id, closing).
  2. **Offline one-step escalation test.**
     - Bank U states are sampled independently of outcome, so Y_BQ − Y_QQ is the realised effect of escalating that decision.
     - A Q-visible trigger (entropy or margin) is chosen on f01 and **frozen by sha before f02/f03 are read**, then evaluated on f02/f03 against a matched-rate random trigger.
     - This is single-decision escalation at sampled states, not a closed-loop policy.
- **Cost:** about 2 GPU-h.
- **Purpose:** this could supply the "concrete mechanism plus validating intervention" the paper currently lacks.
