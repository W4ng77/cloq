\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our method evaluates a precision upgrade as a specified intervention at an exactly restored state, measures the rescues and damages of replacing the action, the continuation or both, and checks them against a same-precision substitute and a meaning-preserving representation change. On our agents these references also change outcomes in both directions, so outcome sensitivity alone does not show that more bits are needed. Evaluating a precision upgrade therefore requires measuring its rescues and damages under the intended continuation policy against reference substitutions, with selection labels that match the executed intervention; our protocol makes these quantities observable and separates outcome sensitivity from evidence of task benefit.
