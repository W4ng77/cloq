\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What an upgrade evaluation should report.} A study of precision routing or escalation should report the net task gain of the intervention it actually executes (the current action only, a continuation takeover, or both, under the continuation that runs afterwards) as rescues and damages, with that intervention's cost; a gain measured for one is not the gain of another (\S\ref{sec:value}). Enrichment of outcome-changing decisions validates a signal's sensitivity, not its value; our trigger result concerns one frozen Q-only rule, and learned routers are untested (\S\ref{sec:r4}). Our experiments measure task value, not cost (simulated quantization; Limitations), so they give no cost-optimal upgrade policy.

\paragraph{How to read flips.} The two references (\S\ref{sec:sameprec}) are interventions on their own populations, not noise floors to subtract from B's turnover. Each branch is one realised outcome under the fixed greedy stack, not an expectation over simulators or semantic perturbations. One hypothesis, not an identified mechanism, is that a change in the history the agent reads propagates through the continuation. We tested one key-order change and specific checkpoint substitutions, not a perturbation curve, so the results do not say that any token change has these consequences.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluate a precision upgrade as a specified intervention at a restored state whose replay is audited, counting the rescues and damages of replacing the action, the continuation or both, against a same-bit-width checkpoint and a key-order change that keeps the tool operation identical. These references also change outcomes (two-sided for the same-bit-width checkpoint; observed in both directions, exploratory, for key order), and at common states B's extra action value over that checkpoint is not resolved; equivalence is not shown. Outcome sensitivity alone therefore does not show that more bits are needed. Upgrade evaluations should report the rescues and damages of the executed intervention under the intended continuation, read them against reference interventions, and validate selection rules by net value, not flip enrichment.
