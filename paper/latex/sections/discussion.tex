\paragraph{When to escalate.} Average success, action divergence and replacement gain disagree here. The four W4 cells have end-to-end differences we can neither distinguish from zero nor bound within $\pm$3\pp{} (Appendix~\ref{app:e2e}), yet replacing their first tool-structure-divergent action turns over many units (\S\ref{sec:r1}). Escalation can also hurt (q4 KV8 $H$, \S\ref{sec:r2}; telecom holdout, \S\ref{sec:r3}). No replacement unit was best in every cell (\S\ref{sec:r2}), so $G$, $H$ and $F$ should be measured per configuration and environment. \pending{[Same-precision reading: one sentence.]} We recommend reporting $r$, $d$ and $M$ next to any net gain, each with its denominator, and keeping fixed-pool and new-task intervals apart. % src: analysis/insights/iter_4/out/success_ni_ALIGNED_20260930T213009Z.md
