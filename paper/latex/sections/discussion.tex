\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Sensitivity and value are different measurements.} Replacing one action turns over many units in both directions for a small net effect, while the end-to-end differences of the four W4 cells can neither be distinguished from zero nor bounded within $\pm$3\pp{} (\S\ref{sec:value}). A second checkpoint at the same bit width, at W4 and at W3, produces sensitivity of comparable size, partly on different units (\S\ref{sec:sameprec}), so a flip at a state shows that the state is fragile, not that it needs more bits; on identical states B's action is shown neither worth more than a second checkpoint's nor equivalent to it. A signal that finds flips does not yet find net gains (\S\ref{sec:r4}). We therefore recommend reporting $M$ and continuation dependence as sensitivity and the net gain with its rescue and harm counts as value, each with its denominator, and reporting the same quantities for a same-precision reference beside any claim about precision.
