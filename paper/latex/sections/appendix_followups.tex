\section{Exploratory Follow-ups: Label Transfer and Takeover Duration}
\label{app:followups}

Both analyses are exploratory, and no main-text claim rests on them. The label-transfer analysis is post hoc. The takeover analysis code was frozen before its outcomes were read, but its configurations were chosen after earlier results had been seen.

\subsection{Transfer of gain labels between upgrade modes}
\label{app:transfer}

\paragraph{Design.} Protocol \texttt{LABEL\_TRANSFER\_POSTHOC.md} (exploratory, post hoc: these outcomes had been seen). For a selection label $X$ and an executed mode $Y$, both in $\{G,H,F\}$, and a budget of 5, 10 or 20\%, we select by $X$ and report the executed rescues, damages and net of $Y$, with ties broken uniformly at random (exact expectation). In the split-half variant, tasks are ranked by mean $X$ on one half of the blocks (outcome-independent hash) and evaluated on the other half, averaged over both directions; the diagonal is a self-label reference, not an upper bound, since it can also do poorly on the evaluation half. Same-draw selection on one set of outcomes is a hindsight diagnostic: its loss is non-negative by construction, so only its gain over random selection is informative. This tests known tasks across nonces, not new tasks or single decisions. Intervals are block and crossed task$\times$block bootstrap, uncorrected for the 18 off-diagonal comparisons per frame. Budgets count states or tasks, not BF16 compute: $H$ and $F$ use several BF16 decisions per selected state. % src: docs/insights/LABEL_TRANSFER_POSTHOC.md; analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md
\paragraph{Results.} The protocol designates ordinary states (Bank U U1, 3 blocks) as the primary frame and first-divergence states (Bank P) as secondary, and designates no budget or label pair. In the primary frame no split-half loss is resolved ($H\!\to\!F$ at 10\%: $+0.30$ [$-0.60$, $+1.11$]), and the self-label references themselves cover zero. In the secondary frame (W4 pool, split-half, pp of all units), at a 10\% task budget, the losses with crossed intervals above zero are $H\!\to\!F$ 2.82 [1.40, 3.90], $H\!\to\!G$ 2.43 [0.97, 3.68] and $G\!\to\!F$ 1.50 [0.20, 2.19]; at 5\%, $H\!\to\!G$ and $H\!\to\!F$ (1.70 each) also exclude zero, and at 20\%, $H\!\to\!F$ 3.39 [1.94, 4.91]. The mode ranking implied by the selection label matches the self-label ranking for $F$ at 10 and 20\% and for $G$ at 5\% (point estimates). Same-draw results, U2B and KV8 pools are in the output file. % src: analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md (Bank P split-half 5/10/20%; Bank U U1 split-half 10%)

\subsection{Takeover duration}
\label{app:takeover}

\paragraph{Results.} Under SpinQuant-W4A4, against Q's own continuation, a one-decision takeover shows no detectable net gain ($0.0$, crossed [$-3.5$, $+3.5$]). Four decisions gain $+6.3$\pp{}: block-$t$ [$+0.2$, $+12.4$] (2 df), crossed [0.0, $+12.2$], so the crossed interval does not confirm it, and an all-state gain alone does not show that value is kept after handback to Q. Four decisions fall $5.5$\pp{} short of sustained takeover ($+11.8$; shortfall block-$t$ [3.8, 7.2], crossed [0.8, 10.6]). Four-decision windows handed control back at 66\% of states; the rest ended inside the window, so they cannot show that four steps suffice. Under GPTQ-W4 the continuation value itself is small ($+0.8$, crossed [$-5.9$, $+7.5$]) and no window effect is resolved. In the selected configuration takeover value depends on its length \citep[cf.\ handoff costs,][]{ganz2026handoff}; GPTQ-W4 does not resolve whether this generalises. % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md (Reading; Handback per window: spinquant_w4a4 Q4 buckets 169/255); analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json (cells.spinquant_w4a4.primary.all_states_pp: t_ci95, crossed_ci95; cells.gptq_w4_d1.primary.all_states_pp.V_Qinf); docs/insights/TAKEOVER_WINDOW_PROTOCOL.md (§8 primary interval; §9; Amendment 3); derived: 66=100*169/255

\paragraph{Design and reading.} Protocol \texttt{TAKEOVER\_WINDOW\_PROTOCOL.md} (tw-v1, exploratory; analysis code frozen before outcomes were read, Amendments 2--4). Both cells were chosen after earlier results were seen: GPTQ-W4 for its unresolved sustained-takeover value, SpinQuant-W4A4 for its large one ($H=+11.8$\pp{} on this panel, Table~\ref{tab:ordinary-modes}); $V_{Q,\infty}$ reuses that endpoint, so the SpinQuant results are conditional on this selection, and no interval is adjusted for this configuration selection. At 255 q8 BF16-sourced states (blocks f01--f03, 85 tasks each), the current action comes from Q (or B); BF16 executes the next $L$ complete decisions and hands back to Q. $V_{Q,L}=Y_{Q,L}-Y_{Q,0}$ is the value of an $L$-decision BF16 window after Q's action, $R_{Q,L}=Y_{Q,L}-Y_{Q,\infty}$ its shortfall against sustained takeover. All six tasks passed acceptance (0 errors, 0 overflows, 256/256 endpoint re-runs reproduced, nothing quarantined). The $E_{Q,4}{=}1$ subset keeps states where continuous BF16 generated more than four decisions, so a four-decision window really hands back. Intervals: block-$t$ over 3 block means (registered primary, df $=2$) and crossed task$\times$block bootstrap (Table~\ref{tab:takeover}). The frozen reading script uses the registered primary interval only; Amendment~3 requires at least two blocks and a nonzero-width interval. It reports the all-state SpinQuant four-decision gain as above zero and states that this alone is not value kept after handback, because states that end inside the window contribute $V_{Q,\infty}$ mechanically. The paper reports each takeover result under both intervals and does not call a result resolved when the crossed interval includes zero, a reading adopted when the results were read, not one fixed in the protocol. The four-decision gain is resolved under block-$t$ but not under the crossed interval both on all states and on the $E_{Q,4}{=}1$ subset. Differences between action sources at each $L$ ($\Delta_L$) are unresolved in both cells. % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md; analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json; docs/insights/TAKEOVER_WINDOW_PROTOCOL.md
