\section{Exploratory Follow-ups: Label Transfer and Takeover Duration}
\label{app:followups}

Both analyses are exploratory, and no main-text claim rests on them. The label-transfer analysis is post hoc. The takeover analysis code was frozen before its outcomes were read, but its configurations were chosen after earlier results had been seen.

\subsection{Transfer of gain labels between upgrade modes}
\label{app:transfer}

\paragraph{Intervention-label transfer.} We rank tasks by one mode's mean gain on half of the nonce blocks and execute a mode on the same tasks in the other half (known tasks across nonces, not new tasks or single decisions). The frozen post hoc protocol designates ordinary states (Bank U, U1) as the primary frame and first divergences (Bank P) as secondary; it fixes no budget or label pair, and we report one selected entry, the continuation label choosing joint replacement at a 10\% task budget (full matrix below; crossed 95\%, uncorrected). In the primary frame, selecting tasks for joint replacement by the continuation label instead of the joint label loses $+0.30$\pp{} [$-0.60$, $+1.11$], not resolved. In the secondary frame, the joint-replacement label gains $+4.35$\pp{} of all units and the continuation label $+1.52$\pp{}, a paired loss of $2.82$\pp{} [1.40, 3.90]. % src: analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md (Bank U U1 PRIMARY split-half budget 10%, row H, column F: loss; Bank P dev_all units SECONDARY split-half budget 10%); docs/insights/LABEL_TRANSFER_POSTHOC.md (§2 frames)

\paragraph{Design.} Protocol \texttt{LABEL\_TRANSFER\_POSTHOC.md} (exploratory, post hoc: these outcomes had been seen). For a selection label $X$ and an executed mode $Y$, both in $\{G,H,F\}$, and a budget of 5, 10 or 20\%, we select by $X$ and report the executed rescues, damages and net of $Y$, with ties broken uniformly at random (exact expectation). In the split-half variant, tasks are ranked by mean $X$ on one half of the blocks (outcome-independent hash) and evaluated on the other half, averaged over both directions; the diagonal is a self-label reference, not an upper bound, since it can also do poorly on the evaluation half. Same-draw selection on one set of outcomes is a hindsight diagnostic: its loss is non-negative by construction, so only its gain over random selection is informative. Intervals are block and crossed task$\times$block bootstrap, uncorrected for the 18 off-diagonal comparisons per frame. Budgets count states or tasks, not BF16 compute: $H$ and $F$ use several BF16 decisions per selected state. % src: docs/insights/LABEL_TRANSFER_POSTHOC.md; analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md
\paragraph{Results.} The protocol designates ordinary states (Bank U U1, 3 blocks) as the primary frame and first-divergence states (Bank P) as secondary, and designates no budget or label pair. In the primary frame no split-half loss is resolved ($H\!\to\!F$ at 10\%: $+0.30$ [$-0.60$, $+1.11$]), and the self-label references themselves cover zero. In the secondary frame (W4 pool, split-half, pp of all units), at a 10\% task budget, the losses with crossed intervals above zero are $H\!\to\!F$ 2.82 [1.40, 3.90], $H\!\to\!G$ 2.43 [0.97, 3.68] and $G\!\to\!F$ 1.50 [0.20, 2.19]; at 5\%, $H\!\to\!G$ and $H\!\to\!F$ (1.70 each) also exclude zero, and at 20\%, $H\!\to\!F$ 3.39 [1.94, 4.91]. The mode ranking implied by the selection label matches the self-label ranking for $F$ at 10 and 20\% and for $G$ at 5\% (point estimates). Same-draw results, U2B and KV8 pools are in the output file. % src: analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md (Bank P split-half 5/10/20%; Bank U U1 split-half 10%)

\subsection{Takeover duration}
\label{app:takeover}

\paragraph{Takeover duration.} On 255 q8 U2B states of the panel of Appendix~\ref{app:panels} ($n_{\mathrm{blk}}=3$), Q's action is followed by $L$ BF16 decisions and then by Q again. SpinQuant-W4A4 was chosen \emph{after} its sustained-takeover value on this panel ($H=+11.8$\pp{}) was seen, and the sustained arm reuses that endpoint, so its results are conditional on that selection; the intervals are not adjusted for this configuration selection. Against Q's own continuation, a one-decision takeover there shows no detectable net gain ($0.0$, crossed [$-3.5$, $+3.5$]). Four decisions gain $+6.3$\pp{}: the registered block-$t$ analysis supports this all-state gain in the selected configuration ([$+0.2$, $+12.4$], 2 df), the crossed interval does not confirm it ([0.0, $+12.2$]), and an all-state gain alone does not show that value is kept after handback to Q (frozen reading; Appendix~\ref{app:takeover}). Four decisions fall $5.5$\pp{} short of sustained takeover ($+11.8$; paired difference block-$t$ [3.8, 7.2], crossed [0.8, 10.6]). % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md (Reading); analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json (cells.spinquant_w4a4.primary.all_states_pp: t_ci95, crossed_ci95); docs/insights/TAKEOVER_WINDOW_PROTOCOL.md (§8 primary interval; §9; Amendment 3)
Four-decision windows handed control back at 66\% of states; the rest ended inside the window, so they cannot show that four steps suffice. Under GPTQ-W4 the continuation value itself is small ($+0.8$, crossed [$-5.9$, $+7.5$]) and no window effect is resolved (Appendix~\ref{app:takeover}). In the selected configuration takeover value depends on its length \citep[cf.\ handoff costs,][]{ganz2026handoff}; GPTQ-W4 does not resolve whether this generalises. % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md (Handback per window: spinquant_w4a4 Q4 buckets 169/255); analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json (cells.gptq_w4_d1.primary.all_states_pp.V_Qinf); derived: 66=100*169/255

\paragraph{Design and reading.} Protocol \texttt{TAKEOVER\_WINDOW\_PROTOCOL.md} (tw-v1, exploratory; analysis code frozen before outcomes were read, Amendments 2--4). Both cells were chosen after earlier results were seen: GPTQ-W4 for its unresolved sustained-takeover value, SpinQuant-W4A4 for its large one ($H=+11.8$\pp{} on this panel, Table~\ref{tab:ordinary-modes}); $V_{Q,\infty}$ reuses that endpoint, so the SpinQuant results are conditional on this selection, and no interval is adjusted for this configuration selection. At 255 q8 BF16-sourced states (blocks f01--f03, 85 tasks each), the current action comes from Q (or B); BF16 executes the next $L$ complete decisions and hands back to Q. $V_{Q,L}=Y_{Q,L}-Y_{Q,0}$ is the value of an $L$-decision BF16 window after Q's action, $R_{Q,L}=Y_{Q,L}-Y_{Q,\infty}$ its shortfall against sustained takeover. All six tasks passed acceptance (0 errors, 0 overflows, 256/256 endpoint re-runs reproduced, nothing quarantined). The $E_{Q,4}{=}1$ subset keeps states where continuous BF16 generated more than four decisions, so a four-decision window really hands back. Intervals: block-$t$ over 3 block means (registered primary, df $=2$) and crossed task$\times$block bootstrap (Table~\ref{tab:takeover}). The frozen reading script uses the registered primary interval only; Amendment~3 requires at least two blocks and a nonzero-width interval. It reports the all-state SpinQuant four-decision gain as above zero and states that this alone is not value kept after handback, because states that end inside the window contribute $V_{Q,\infty}$ mechanically. The paper reports each takeover result under both intervals and does not call a result resolved when the crossed interval includes zero, a reading adopted when the results were read, not one fixed in the protocol. The four-decision gain is resolved under block-$t$ but not under the crossed interval both on all states and on the $E_{Q,4}{=}1$ subset. Differences between action sources at each $L$ ($\Delta_L$) are unresolved in both cells. % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md; analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json; docs/insights/TAKEOVER_WINDOW_PROTOCOL.md
