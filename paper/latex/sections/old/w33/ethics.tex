\section*{Ethics Statement}

This work involves no crowdworkers and no personal data: all users are simulated by an LLM on the public \tautwo{} tasks, and all agent models are openly released Qwen3 checkpoints. The planned human review of about 80 fork states (\S\ref{sec:annotation}) concerns synthetic benchmark conversations only. \pending{who the reviewers are and their instructions --- to be fixed with the annotation guidelines} The main risk we see is over-reading: a small or cancelling one-replacement gain on one simulator and a fixed task pool does not certify a quantized agent for deployment, and non-inferiority at a 3-point margin is not equivalence. Deployment decisions in consequential settings should not rest on these measurements alone. Compute use is reported in the Responsible NLP checklist.
