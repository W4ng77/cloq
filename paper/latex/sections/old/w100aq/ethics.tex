\section*{Ethics Statement}

This work involves no crowdworkers and no personal data. All users are simulated by an LLM on public benchmark tasks, and all agents are openly released Qwen3, Mistral-Small-3.2 and (gate check only) Llama-3.1 checkpoints, or simulated-quantized versions of them. % nocheck: model names
 The process annotations were produced by LLMs on synthetic benchmark conversations. The main risk we see is over-reading. A small forked-state gain on one simulator and a fixed task pool does not certify a quantized agent for deployment, and our end-to-end comparisons do not establish equivalence. Deployment decisions in consequential settings should not rest on these measurements alone.
