\section{Robustness and Diagnostics}
\label{sec:diagnostics}

\paragraph{The determinism incident as a finding.} In the legacy pass, the q4 determinism pair served on two nodes disagreed on 20/20 tasks and 113/113 identical-prompt decisions, because torch Inductor autotuned the reduction configuration of the fused residual + RMSNorm kernel at compile time and so changed the summation order, which vLLM's batch invariance does not cover (Appendix~\ref{app:extra}). The effect was that of any perturbation above the floor: median $\absd{} = 0.125$, non-zero shifts on 59\% of tokens, KM $P(\le 4 / \le 12) = 0.50 / 0.95$ over 20 tasks, and 4/20 outcomes changed ($p = 0.63$). Deterministic Inductor compilation plus a numerics canary removed it (\S\ref{sec:setup}). Stacks that compile kernels may be exposed to it. Harness length, simulator-side loops and a strict error budget \citep{jang2026flat} do not change our conclusions on the data available; real quantized kernels, the strict endpoint on the damage cells, and eager nulls for the larger models are pending (Appendix~\ref{app:robust}).
