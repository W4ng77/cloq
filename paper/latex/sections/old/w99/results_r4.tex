\section{R4: Can Beneficial Replacements Be Found in Advance?}
\label{sec:r4}

$G$, $H$ and $F$ are oracle quantities that require running B and all four branches; a deployable rule sees only Q. Following \citet{lotfi2026quantized}, we scored every Bank U f01 state at the token level (1140 cell $\times$ state rows, 58 flips), teacher-forcing both models on the generated ids of $a_{\mathrm{B}}$ and $a_{\mathrm{Q}}$. We record truncated top-20 entropy, the top-1 margin and a top-20-union KL, not full entropy or KL. % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261001T185031Z.md (§0); docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W88
All analyses are exploratory, and f01 is development data.

\paragraph{Uncertainty is associated with divergence.} Q's entropy at the start of its action separates states where B and Q act differently from states where they act identically, with AUC 0.66--0.91 per sub-bank and model. Over all ordinary states, flips also sit at lower Q margins (U1: AUC 0.31 [0.22, 0.40] for q4, 0.24 [0.13, 0.36] for q8; stratified). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261001T185031Z.md (§1a, §1b "differing vs exact"); docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W88

\paragraph{Among divergent states, it does not reliably identify consequences.} Restricting to states where the actions differ removes the ``any difference at all'' signal. In U1 the signal is not detectable: no Q-visible statistic has an interval excluding 0.5 (21 and 9 flips; f01). Only small sub-banks keep a signal: q4 U2B (mean entropy over $a_{\mathrm{Q}}$, AUC 0.73 [0.53, 0.89]) and q4 U2Q (entropy and margin at the action start, AUC 0.90 and 0.10; 10 states, 4 flips). At the first divergent token, pooled entropy and KL do not separate flips from non-flips except in q4 U2Q (KL AUC 0.80 [0.65, 0.93], entropy 0.25 [0.07, 0.48]; 4 flips). Per cell, two further unadjusted intervals exclude 0.5, in opposite directions (U1 q4 GPTQ-W4 entropy 0.30; U2B q4 KV8 KL 0.10). No class of first divergent token reproduces the marker enrichment of \citet{lotfi2026quantized}. The largest q4 enrichment is ordinary word choice (rate ratio 1.89, Fisher $p=.019$, not corrected). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261001T185031Z.md (§1a differing_only, §1b, §2); docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W88
On development data, uncertainty is associated with \emph{where} quantization changes the action more strongly than with \emph{which} changes matter.

\paragraph{A frozen trigger, tested on held-out blocks.} We froze a Q-visible trigger on f01 before any f02/f03 state was scored: escalate one decision when the minimum Q margin over $a_{\mathrm{Q}}$ is low, with per-cell thresholds at 5, 10 and 20\% escalation rates (Appendix~\ref{app:oracle}). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261001T185031Z.md (§3); docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W88
On held-out f02/f03 (1020 U1 rows, 69 turnover states) it escalated 50, 114 and 222 states. Net gains were $+4$, $+2$ and $+5$ (rescues/damages 6/2, 7/5, 16/11) against matched-count random expectations of $+0.21$, $+0.98$ and $+2.38$ (exact one-sided hypergeometric $p=.0345$, $.417$, $.262$). It captured 8, 12 and 27 turnover states against 3.48, 7.52 and 14.30 expected ($p=.0182$, $.0613$, $.0002$). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261002T003130Z.md (§3 Validation, U1 S_mm PRIMARY)
With no level fixed in advance, net gain passes a nominal one-sided .05 only at the 5\% rate, and not after Holm correction.
The frozen rule thus finds outcome-changing states but has no significant net advantage over random selection, and its held-out blocks reuse the development tasks.

\paragraph{Headroom.} A hindsight action-replacement oracle with the same per-cell counts nets $+3.33$, $+3.73$ and $+3.73$\pp{}. This headroom is positive by construction: the oracle picks only rescues (padding with neutral states) and never a damage. The rule captures 4/34, 2/38 and 5/38 of that net. A menu oracle and a post-hoc KL$(\mathrm{B}\|\mathrm{Q})$ row (not Q-only; no significant net gain, Holm $p=1.0$) are in Appendix~\ref{app:oracle} (Figure~\ref{fig:oracle}). % src: analysis/fork/out/oracle_trigger_20261002T024423Z.md (§1a, §2)
