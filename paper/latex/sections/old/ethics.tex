\section*{Ethics Statement}

This work involves no human subjects, crowdworkers or personal data: all users are simulated by an LLM on the public \tautwo{} retail and airline tasks, and all agent models are openly released Qwen3 checkpoints. The main risk we see is misuse of the criterion as a certificate. A compressed agent that is not distinguishable from its reference's perturbation null on 85 tasks may still fail on inputs or failure channels that the benchmark does not cover, and at this sample size non-rejection does not establish equivalence. Deployment decisions for compressed agents in consequential settings should therefore not rest on this test alone. Compute use is reported in the Responsible NLP checklist.
