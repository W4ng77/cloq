\paragraph{Large outcome changes, small net estimate.} The development panel has 85 tasks and 20 complete blocks per configuration. We replace Q's action at its first divergence with B's and let Q continue. This changes 15.5--20.9\% of outcomes, with rescues and damages of similar size (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}a). Two fixed-pool intervals of the net exclude zero (negative for q4 GPTQ-W4, positive for q8 GPTQ-W4); no new-task interval does. Pooled over the four W4 configurations, the full-pool net gain $\Gall$ is $+0.7$\pp{} (fixed pool [$-0.3$, $+1.6$]; new tasks [$-2.1$, $+3.4$]). The net is a local replacement effect, not an end-to-end comparison (Appendix~\ref{app:e2e}). % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md (cells; W4:all pooled row: G block bootstrap, M); analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.json (pooled.W4:all:pooled_equal_weight.G.crossed_ci95)

\paragraph{A negative holdout estimate.} The holdout tests 34 unseen telecom tasks over 12 fresh blocks and three W4 configurations. Its protocol was fixed before any task ran. Replacement again produces both rescues and damages (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}b). The primary full-pool net gain $\Gall$, $-4.57$\pp{} (crossed 90\% [$-7.17$, $-1.96$]), lies below zero: action-only replacement reduced success on this holdout. Its cause is unknown. The frozen readings had only positive-gain and small-effect categories, a design omission, so the protocol classifies the result as \emph{inconclusive} (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}b; Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). Both pre-registered secondary checks passed: rescues and damages were each positive, and task fragility repeated across independent prompt blocks ($\rho=0.588$, one-sided permutation $p=0.0001$). % src: analysis/fork/out/holdout_confirm_20261002T012353Z.md (TL;DR; Readings; S1; S2); docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §0, §8
We subsequently replayed QQ at all 1202 divergent states. Every episode matched its original, verifying the shared baseline. The audit protocol was fixed before these replays ran. The holdout changes domain as well as tasks (Appendix~\ref{app:holdoutdev}). % src: analysis/fork/out/holdout_qqfull_audit_20261006T061033Z.md (TL;DR); docs/insights/HOLDOUT_QQFULL_PROTOCOL.md (§1-§5, Amendment 1); docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §1

\paragraph{The intervention defines which rescues count.} Figure~\ref{fig:rd}c reports all three modes on the W4 and KV8 divergent-state pools. For W4, $G$, $H$ and $F$ average $+0.5$, $+0.2$ and $+0.7$\pp{}, respectively; all three new-task intervals cover zero. Similar averages still hide different successes. Joint rescue, where only changing both succeeds, occurs on 4.0\% of W4 divergent states. Rescue undone, where B's action succeeds with Q continuing but fails with B continuing, occurs on 4.8\%. These are observed shares, not evidence of enrichment over a permutation reference (Appendix~\ref{app:offdiag}). Action-only evaluation misses the former; joint-replacement evaluation misses the latter. Thus a measured gain for one mode cannot stand in for another.
