\paragraph{Two-sided turnover, small net gain.} Table~\ref{tab:r1} reports the current-action swap with its denominators. On the \tautwo{} panel (85 tasks $\times$ 20 complete blocks), replacing a W4 agent's first divergent action changes the task outcome in $M=15.5$--$20.9$\% of units. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
Rescues and damages are of similar size, $r=6.8$--$11.4$ and $d=7.5$--$10.1$\pp{}, so the net gain $\Gall$ lies between $-1.9$ and $+2.9$\pp{}. Two fixed-pool intervals exclude zero, negative for q4 GPTQ-W4 and positive for q8 GPTQ-W4; none of the new-task intervals does. Pooled over the four W4 cells, $\Gall$ is $+0.7$ [$-0.3$, $+1.6$]\pp{} with $M=18.6$\%. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
KV8 turns over fewer outcomes ($M=6.5$--$8.1$\%), and the stress cells reach W4-like turnover with only three blocks. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
A small net gain thus coexists with substantial two-sided turnover. $\Gall$ is not an end-to-end success comparison, which on the same checkpoints is inconclusive (Appendix~\ref{app:e2e}).

\paragraph{Turnover concentrates in tasks (exploratory).} The top 20\% of tasks hold 57--71\% of all flips in the W4 and KV8 cells, and task-level turnover and its direction repeat across disjoint halves of the nonce blocks. % src: analysis/fork/out/consequence_v2_20261001T070850Z.json (share_of_consequences_in_top20pct_tasks)
