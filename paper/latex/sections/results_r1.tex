\paragraph{Two-sided turnover, small net gain.} On the \tautwo{} panel (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}; all cells in Table~\ref{tab:r1}; 85 tasks $\times$ 20 complete blocks), replacing a W4 agent's first tool-structure-divergent action changes the task outcome in $M=15.5$--$20.9$\% of units. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
Rescues and damages are of similar size, $r=6.8$--$11.4$ and $d=7.5$--$10.1$\pp{}, so the net gain $\Gall$ lies between $-1.9$ and $+2.9$\pp{}. Two fixed-pool intervals exclude zero (negative for q4 GPTQ-W4, positive for q8 GPTQ-W4); no new-task interval does. Pooled over the four W4 cells, $\Gall$ is $+0.7$ [$-0.3$, $+1.6$]\pp{} with $M=18.6$\%. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
KV8 cells turn over fewer outcomes ($M=6.5$--$8.1$\%; a descriptive comparison across cells), and four of the five stress cells reach W4-like turnover ($M=18.4$--$20.4\%$) with only three blocks; q8 RTN-W3, whose own success is low, turns over $6.7\%$. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
$\Gall$ is a local replacement effect, not an end-to-end success comparison (Appendix~\ref{app:e2e}).

\paragraph{Turnover concentrates in tasks.} In an exploratory, retrospective analysis of the development data, the top 20\% of tasks hold 57--71\% of all flips in the W4 and KV8 cells, and task-level turnover and its direction repeat across disjoint halves of the nonce blocks (Appendix~\ref{app:concentration}). % src: analysis/fork/out/consequence_v2_20261001T070850Z.json (share_of_consequences_in_top20pct_tasks); analysis/fork/out/direction_replication_v2_20261001T0600Z_20261001T070500Z.json (internal replication, viewed data)
On unseen telecom tasks, the pre-registered repeatability test (S2) passed: per-task turnover in one half of the nonce blocks predicts turnover in the other half better than persona-preserving random pairing ($\rho=0.59$, $p=.0001$; \S\ref{sec:r3}). S2 tests neither concentration nor direction, and it does not rule out task difficulty as the explanation. % src: analysis/fork/out/holdout_confirm_20261002T012353Z.md
