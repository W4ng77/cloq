\paragraph{Rescues and damages are both large; the pooled net is not resolved.} On the \tautwo{} development panel (85 tasks $\times$ 20 complete blocks), replacing a W4 agent's action at the first divergent state with B's, while Q continues, changes the task outcome in 15.5--20.9\% of units (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}a). Rescues (6.8--11.4\pp{}) and damages (7.5--10.1\pp{}) are of similar size, so the net lies between $-1.9$ and $+2.9$\pp{}. Two fixed-pool intervals exclude zero (negative for q4 GPTQ-W4, positive for q8 GPTQ-W4); no new-task interval does. The net is a local replacement effect, not an end-to-end comparison (Appendix~\ref{app:e2e}). % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
Pooled over the four W4 configurations, the net is $+0.7$\pp{} (fixed pool [$-0.3$, $+1.6$]; new tasks [$-2.1$, $+3.4$]) and the turnover 18.6\%. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md (W4:all pooled row: G block bootstrap, M); analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.json (pooled.W4:all:pooled_equal_weight.G.crossed_ci95)

\paragraph{Which mode is evaluated decides which rescues are counted.} On divergent states no average gain is detected for upgrading this step, the rest, or both (W4 pool: $G=+0.5$, $H=+0.2$, $F=+0.7$\pp{}; all fixed-pool and new-task intervals cover zero; Appendix~\ref{app:r2main}). These average over divergent states only, so $G$ is not the full-pool net above. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1 pools, W4:all); analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.json
The averages hide states where single-mode evaluations disagree. In a \emph{joint rescue} only BB succeeds (Figure~\ref{fig:case}); these are 4.0\% of W4 divergent states (fixed-pool 95\% [3.5, 4.5]; new-task [2.7, 5.3]), and testing only the current action ($G$) misses them. In a \emph{rescue undone}, B's action rescues under Q's continuation but fails under B's continuation; these are 4.8\% (fixed-pool [4.1, 5.4]; new-task [3.2, 6.5]), and testing only the joint replacement ($F$) misses them. % src: analysis/fork/out/outcome_classes_20261004T143614Z.md
A label-permutation reference that keeps how many branches succeed produces both patterns at similar rates, so their frequency gives no evidence of enrichment that generalises to new tasks (exploratory; Appendix~\ref{app:offdiag}). Whatever their cause, a gain measured for action replacement ($G$) cannot be reused as the gain of the joint replacement ($F$), or the reverse. % src: analysis/fork/out/pattern_ref_20261005T134721Z.md (§1, pool W4:all)

\paragraph{On held-out telecom tasks the net estimate is negative, and the frozen reading is inconclusive.} On 34 unseen telecom tasks, under a protocol frozen before any holdout unit ran (12 fresh blocks, three W4 configurations), the replacement again rescued (5.2\pp{}) and damaged (9.8\pp{}). The primary net, $-4.57$\pp{} (crossed 90\% [$-7.17$, $-1.96$]), lies below zero. The frozen readings had only positive-gain and small-effect categories, a design omission, so the protocol classifies the result as \emph{inconclusive} (Figure~\ref{fig:rd}b; Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). Both pre-registered secondary checks passed: S1 (rescues and damages both positive) and S2 (cross-half repeatability of task fragility, $\rho=0.588$, one-sided permutation $p=0.0001$). % src: analysis/fork/out/holdout_confirm_20261002T012353Z.md (TL;DR; Readings; S1; S2); docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §0, §8
All 135 QQ no-op replays selected by the fixed 10\% hash rule, of 1202 divergent states, matched; this covers only the sampled states. The holdout changes domain as well as tasks (Appendix~\ref{app:holdoutdev}). % src: analysis/fork/out/holdout_noop_audit_20261004T152207Z.md (TL;DR); docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §1
