\section*{Limitations}

The evidence has clear boundaries. We use two \tautwo{} domains and 85 tasks, so run-level standard errors are about 3 points. The pre-registered minimum detectable effect of about 3.4\pp{} applies to the pooled five-model free-zone test; single-family tests on one model with 2--4 draws have less power, and because we have not run a declared-margin equivalence test, a $\Delta$ whose interval includes zero shows only that the cell is not distinguishable from the null, not that it is equivalent. All decoding is greedy; under sampling, sampling noise would dominate the null and the ``one draw'' issue would change form. Quantized cells are fake-quantized, and real-kernel checks are pending. One fixed user simulator is used for the main matrix (the same model as the q30a3 agent); a different simulator, or simulator-side perturbations, may change null widths. All models are from one family (Qwen3). The legacy pass is variant-mixed, and several numbers pool legacy and clean draws on the assumption that the numerics variant is an exchangeable perturbation, an assumption that itself rests on P1. The clean-pass numbers are live snapshots taken while runs were in progress. The 4B deep-dive numbers were recomputed from analysis files, not from raw runs. The closed-loop median \absd{} is quantized on the bf16 grid, and its smallest non-zero value is the grid step at which we locate the floor; no perturbation below one grid step was tested. The open-loop \absd{} is a different quantity that we never compare numerically with it, and matched-shift comparisons are provisional until one definition is used throughout.

\paragraph{Open questions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
\item Does the divergence floor reached at the grid step hold for all five models, and does its height follow each model's text-tie density? (\S\ref{sec:p1}, pending)
\item Does the q4 damage threshold survive on clean cells alone, and does it appear on q8 and the larger models, whose base rates differ? (\S\ref{sec:p2}, pending)
\item Is the q8 RTN-W4 deficit at matched shift real, and if so, what property of RTN error causes it? (\S\ref{sec:p3}, at risk)
\item Is the write-flip rate the right predictor of damage, as the recovery asymmetry in the 4B study (0.21--0.35 when the diverging action is a write versus 0.40--0.80 for a read or lookup) suggests? (\S\ref{sec:p2}, pending)
\item Is the $+5.1$\pp{} of the Gaussian $k{=}0.03$ family above the null a chance fluctuation, a canonical-prompt effect against nonces, or an effect of the mix of passes in the family and the null? (\S\ref{sec:p2}, pending)
\item Does \tautwo{}'s success check count empty or terminated responses as successes in our damage cells, as the Agentic Benchmark Checklist reports for $\tau$-bench \citep{zhu2025abc}? (not audited)
\item How do our 16--27\% outcome flips under numerics-only changes relate to the Replay Gap \citep{gonuguntla2026replay}, whose abstract reports no outcome flips across 359 same-model control forks? Candidate differences are the simulator in the loop, bf16 ties in free text, and the binary database endpoint; we have not tested them.
\end{itemize}
