\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{What we do not claim.} We do not claim success equivalence between BF16 and W4 agents; equivalence tests at $\pm$3\pp{} on new-task intervals fail. % src: analysis/insights/iter_4/out/success_ni_ALIGNED_20260930T213009Z.md
$G$, $H$ and $F$ are oracle quantities, not the value of a deployable policy (\S\ref{sec:r4}). We do not claim a W$\times$KV interaction, and we do not present static precision restoration as a method (Appendix~\ref{app:withdrawn}).

\paragraph{One model family, two sizes.} All agents are Qwen3 models (4B and 8B). Effects may differ at other scales and in other families. End-to-end runs exist for larger Qwen models, but they have no crossed forks, and we draw no conclusion from them (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}).

\paragraph{Simulated quantization.} Quantized checkpoints are dequantized and served with BF16 compute on a deterministic path. Deployed low-bit kernels change the numerics and add serving variation that we deliberately remove. We make no claims about latency, memory or deployed kernels, and the cost of a BF16 fallback is outside our scope.

\paragraph{One user simulator; two small controls.} All \tautwo{} episodes use a single greedy simulator, and continuation effects include its replies. Both second-simulator controls replace only the user after a fixed history, use a stochastic Sonnet 5.5 user (a subagent relay for Bank U, isolated single calls for Bank P), cover 78 and 80 states, and say nothing about real users. The Bank P control has 20 states per cell, too few to confirm or refute few-pp contrasts. We did not run a same-precision perturbation $2\times2$ (e.g.\ B versus B under another nonce), so the off-diagonal share is not calibrated against controller-independent sensitivity: in this deterministic setting it includes sensitivity of the continuation to any change of controller. % src: docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W91, W97; analysis/fork/out/simctrl_p_20261002T015200Z.md

\paragraph{LLM annotation.} Process labels in Appendix~\ref{app:injection} come from LLM annotators (Sonnet 5.5, two independent instances plus an adjudicator) and rule-based checks. No human annotation has been done. Agreement among instances of one model family shows reproducibility, not correctness. % src: analysis/annotation/wp5/table4_v2.md

\paragraph{Small confirmation and partial banks.} The pre-registered holdout covers 34 telecom tasks and three cells. Its negative estimate is established only for telecom, these three cells and this simulator, and its pre-specified readings did not include a negative-gain category. % src: docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §1
Bank P intervenes only at the first tool-structure divergence per task and block. Bank U samples one state per task and block and has three blocks, so its block intervals rest on two degrees of freedom. Prompt nonces define replicate blocks; they are a researcher-chosen perturbation, not a deployment distribution.
