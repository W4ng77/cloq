\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{One model family, two sizes.} All agents are Qwen3 models (4B and 8B). Effects may differ at other scales and in other families. End-to-end runs exist for larger Qwen models, but they have no crossed forks, and we draw no conclusion from them (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}).

\paragraph{Simulated quantization.} Quantized checkpoints are dequantized and served with BF16 compute on a deterministic path. Deployed low-bit kernels change the numerics and add serving variation that we deliberately remove. We make no claims about latency, memory or deployed kernels, and the cost of a BF16 fallback is outside our scope.

\paragraph{One user simulator; a relay control.} All \tautwo{} episodes use a single greedy simulator model, and continuation effects include its replies. The second-simulator control uses Sonnet 5.5 answering through a Claude Code subagent relay, which is stochastic and whose context we do not fully control. % src: docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W91 It re-runs continuations on histories produced with the original simulator, covers only Bank U text flips at one block, and tests neither Bank P nor behaviour with real users. Its result, that text-difference outcome labels do not reproduce reliably under it, may also apply in part to Bank P states that involve text, which we have not tested.

\paragraph{LLM annotation.} Process labels in Appendix~\ref{app:injection} come from LLM annotators (Sonnet 5.5, two independent instances plus an adjudicator) and rule-based checks. No human annotation has been done. % src: analysis/annotation/wp5/table4_v2.md Agreement among instances of one model family shows reproducibility, not correctness.

\paragraph{Small confirmation and partial banks.} The pre-registered holdout covers 34 telecom tasks and three cells. Its negative average gain is specific to telecom, these cells and this simulator, and its pre-specified readings did not include a negative-gain category. % src: docs/insights/PREREG_FORK_CONFIRM.md §1
Bank P intervenes only at the first tool-structure divergence per task and block. Bank U samples one state per task and block and has three blocks, so its block intervals rest on two degrees of freedom. Prompt nonces define replicate blocks; they are a researcher-chosen perturbation, not a deployment distribution.
