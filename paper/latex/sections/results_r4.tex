\section{When Upgrade Value Appears, Disappears or Moves}
\label{sec:scope}
\label{sec:r3}

Upgrade value can appear, disappear, or shift between the action and the continuation. In \S\ref{sec:value}, without a simulated user (BFCL, ALFWorld) and on the pre-registered telecom holdout, the replacement rescues and damages and its net value is small or negative; in one exploratory configuration (Qwen3-14B, BFCL) value can appear in the action, and in a three-block stress cell (q8 RTN-W3) it can move to the continuation. Stress cells are kept separate from W4 (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}).

Three further lines vary numerical quality, cache location and model family. They use ordinary BF16-sourced states (Bank U U2B, \S\ref{sec:method}), drawn outcome-blind from the BF16 model's own trajectories and shared by every method of that model; they follow the BF16 policy's state distribution, not the one a quantized agent reaches. Methods are compared on these states by $D_{cc}=\YY{QB}-\YY{BB}$, Q's action against B's under the shared BF16 continuation (details: Appendix~\ref{app:panels}). % src: docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (§2.1, §4)

\paragraph{Numerical quality: better local numerics, no detected task value.} Of two rotation-based quantizers \citep{ashkboos2024quarot,liu2025spinquant}, SpinQuant-W4A4 is closer to BF16 than QuaRot-W4A4 on the shared q8 states (three blocks; mean token-level KL 0.165 against 0.199; descriptive, no interval), but its task value is not shown to differ: the pre-registered matched contrast is $+1.2$ [$-3.9$, $+6.2$]\pp{} (Holm-adjusted $p=1.0$; one block rests on a re-run). Across the eleven q8 cells, mean KL orders the cells' turnover almost perfectly (Spearman $\rho=0.92$, descriptive): local fidelity predicts how much a configuration changes outcomes, not what an upgrade gains. Value appears only where activations are also quantized: in SpinQuant-W4A4, B's takeover is worth $H=+11.8$ [6.7, 16.8] and full substitution $F=+13.7$ [9.3, 18.2]\pp{}, while B's action alone is not resolved ($G=+3.1$ [$-2.9$, $+9.2$]). % src: analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.md; analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.json (tau2.cells.*.all_states_pp; tau2.contrasts; fidelity.cells.*.KLu_mean; fidelity.across_cells_spearman_KLu_mean_vs_M_cc); docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (A1.2, A4.4, A7, A7.E1, A7.E2)

\paragraph{Cache location: no detected shift of value.} KIVI \citep{liu2024kivi} quantizes keys per channel and values per token; we use a replay-compatible variant. KIVI-2 against KV8 is not resolved ($-0.6$ [$-38.0$, $+36.8$]\pp{}), nor is KIVI-4 against KV8 ($+1.6$ [$-2.9$, $+6.0$]\pp{}). The KIVI-2 contrast rests on two blocks, both depending on re-runs of arms lost to server failures (strict rule: not estimable), and the unquantized kernel path itself reproduces B's action in 85.1\% of states. % src: analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.md; analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.json (tau2.cells.*.all_states_pp; tau2.contrasts; fidelity.cells.*.KLu_mean; fidelity.across_cells_spearman_KLu_mean_vs_M_cc); docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (A1.2, A4.4, A7, A7.E1, A7.E2); analysis/fork/kivi_gate/GATE.md (§6)

\paragraph{Model family: two-sided turnover on Mistral, no resolved net value.} On Mistral-Small-3.2-24B, at its own ordinary BF16-sourced states, replacing Q's action with B's again flips outcomes in both directions with no resolved net value. The estimates are conditional on the 83 tasks with a complete, recoverable BF16 source state in every block, fixed by a dated protocol amendment (GPTQ-W4 on six blocks, RTN-W4 on five). In GPTQ-W4, B's action rescues $r=4.6$ [2.4, 6.8] and damages $d=5.4$ [2.4, 8.5]\pp{} of states ($M=10.0$ [7.0, 13.1]; fixed-pool 95\%), and no mode's net value is resolved ($G=-0.8$ [$-5.2$, $+3.6$], $H=+1.4$ [$-3.0$, $+5.8$], $F=+0.0$ [$-4.2$, $+4.2$]). RTN-W4 is similar ($M=10.4$ [6.3, 14.4]). The pre-registered contrast GPTQ $-$ RTN is not resolved: $\Delta D_{cc}=+2.2$ [$-3.5$, $+7.8$]\pp{}, $p=.35$, on five blocks, two of which are admitted only by a scoring rule adopted after an interim look (Appendix~\ref{app:panels}). % src: analysis/fork/out/newmethod_m24_20261004T024453Z.md; analysis/fork/out/newmethod_m24_20261004T024453Z.json (tau2.cells.*.all_states_pp; tau2.contrasts.M1); analysis/fork/out/m24_acceptance_review_A.md; docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (A3, A6, A6.E1); docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W99p; analysis/fork/ext_l8_gate/GATE.md
