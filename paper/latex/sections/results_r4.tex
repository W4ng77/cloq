\paragraph{Takeover duration.} On 255 q8 U2B states of the \S\ref{sec:r4} KL panel (3 blocks; exploratory), Q's action is followed by $L$ BF16 decisions and then by Q again. Under SpinQuant-W4A4, BF16's continuation is worth $+11.8$\pp{} when it runs to the end (crossed 95\% [5.1, 18.4]), but one BF16 decision before handback is worth $0.0$ [$-3.5$, $+3.5$], and four decisions $+6.3$ [0.0, 12.2], still $5.5$\pp{} short of sustained takeover [$-10.6$, $-0.8$]. % src: analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.md (Reading); analysis/fork/out/takeover_20261005T1604Z.json (cells.spinquant_w4a4.primary.all_states_pp, crossed_ci95)
Four-decision windows handed control back at 66\% of states; the rest ended inside the window, so they cannot show that four steps suffice. Under GPTQ-W4 the continuation value itself is small ($+0.8$ [$-5.9$, $+7.5$]) and no window effect is resolved (Appendix~\ref{app:takeover}).
