\section{Where the Separation Holds or Changes}
\label{sec:scope}
\label{sec:r3}

Each further configuration asks whether sensitivity and value separate as in the main cells (full matrices in the appendix). Without a simulated user (BFCL, ALFWorld) and on unseen telecom tasks, the separation holds: turnover is two-sided and the net value is small or negative (\S\ref{sec:value}).

\paragraph{A larger model: value appears.} On BFCL, Qwen3-14B GPTQ-W4 gives $G=+5.7$ [$+2.9$, $+8.7$], $H=+0.4$\pp{} and 6.4\% continuation-dependent states over 471 divergent items. Here replacing the action has a clearly positive net value, while continuation dependence is at the low end of the BFCL range; this is a single Qwen3 configuration. % src: analysis/fork/out/ext_q14_20261002T085647Z.md (§1, §2, §3)

\paragraph{Lower bit widths: value moves to the continuation.} We keep W3 and rotated KV4 separate from W4 as boundary checks (the W3 same-precision check is in \S\ref{sec:sameprec}). Four of the five stress cells turn over 18.4--20.4\% of units with three blocks; in q8 RTN-W3, whose own success is low, B's continuation is what pays ($H=+15.3$, $F=+18.4$ against $G=-0.4$\pp{}). W2 runs did not complete and are not reported (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}). % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md; analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1)
