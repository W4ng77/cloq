\section{When Upgrade Value Appears, Disappears or Moves}
\label{sec:scope}
\label{sec:r3}

Each further configuration asks how a factor changes this structure: whether upgrade value appears, disappears, or shifts between the action and the continuation. The three lines still running vary numerical quality (QuaRot and SpinQuant), where quantization error lives (KIVI's KV cache) and the model family (Mistral). Full matrices are in the appendix.

\paragraph{Without a simulated user, and on unseen tasks, value stays small while turnover stays two-sided.} On BFCL and ALFWorld, and on the pre-registered telecom holdout, the replacement rescues and damages and its net value is small or negative (\S\ref{sec:value}).

\paragraph{At a larger scale, value can appear in the action.} For Qwen3-14B on BFCL, replacing the action produces net task gain and continuation dependence is low (\S\ref{sec:value}, Figure~\ref{fig:modes}); it is one configuration within the family.

\paragraph{At lower bit widths, value can move to the continuation.} In q8 RTN-W3 the gain comes from B taking over the continuation, not from the action (Figure~\ref{fig:modes}). Four of the five stress cells turn over 18.4--20.4\% of units with three blocks; we keep them separate from W4, and W2 runs did not complete and are not reported (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}). % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md

\paragraph{Cache location: does KV-cache quantization move value to the continuation?} KIVI \citep{liu2024kivi} quantizes keys per channel and values per token. Our implementation is a replay-compatible variant: at every decode step it reconstructs the quantized view that official KIVI's token-by-token decode would hold at that position from the full-precision cache, rather than persisting a compressed cache across turns, so every token, prompt or generated, attends with that view.
