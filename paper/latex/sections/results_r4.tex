\section{When Upgrade Value Appears, Disappears or Moves}
\label{sec:scope}
\label{sec:r3}

Each further factor may change this structure, making upgrade value appear, disappear, or shift between the action and the continuation; three lines still running vary numerical quality, cache location and model family.

\paragraph{What is established.} Without a simulated user (BFCL, ALFWorld) and on the pre-registered telecom holdout, the replacement rescues and damages and its net value is small or negative; at a larger scale (Qwen3-14B, BFCL) value can appear in the action, and at a lower bit width (q8 RTN-W3) it can move to the continuation (\S\ref{sec:value}). Four of the five stress cells turn over 18.4--20.4\% of units with three blocks; we keep them separate from W4, and W2 runs did not complete (Appendix~\ref{app:coverage}). % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md

\paragraph{Numerical quality: better local numerics, no detected task value.} Rotation-based quantizers improve local numerics \citep{ashkboos2024quarot,liu2025spinquant}. On the shared q8 states (three blocks), SpinQuant-W4A4 is closer to BF16 than QuaRot-W4A4 (mean token-level KL 0.165 against 0.199; descriptive, no interval). Its task value is not shown to differ: the matched contrast is $+1.2$ [$-3.9$, $+6.2$]\pp{} (P2; Holm-adjusted $p=1.0$), and QuaRot-W4 against GPTQ-W4 is $-0.4$ [$-10.7$, $+9.9$]\pp{} (P1). Across the eleven q8 cells, mean KL orders the cells' turnover almost perfectly (Spearman $\rho=0.92$, descriptive): local fidelity predicts how much a configuration changes outcomes, not what an upgrade gains. Value appears only where activations are also quantized: in SpinQuant-W4A4, B's takeover is worth $H=+11.8$ [6.7, 16.8] and full substitution $F=+13.7$ [9.3, 18.2]\pp{}, while B's action alone is not resolved ($G=+3.1$ [$-2.9$, $+9.2$]). P2's third block rests on a one-time re-run of arms lost to a user-simulator overload; on the two blocks without it the contrast is $+2.4$\pp{} (strict view). % src: analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.md; analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.json (tau2.cells.*.all_states_pp; tau2.contrasts; fidelity.cells.*.KLu_mean; fidelity.across_cells_spearman_KLu_mean_vs_M_cc); docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (A1.2, A4.4, A7, A7.E1, A7.E2)

\paragraph{Cache location: no detected shift of value.} KIVI \citep{liu2024kivi} quantizes keys per channel and values per token. Our implementation is a replay-compatible variant: at every decode step it reconstructs the quantized view that official KIVI's token-by-token decode would hold at that position from the full-precision cache, rather than persisting a compressed cache across turns, so every token, prompt or generated, attends with that view. KIVI-2 against KV8 is not resolved ($-0.6$ [$-38.0$, $+36.8$]\pp{}, P3), nor is KIVI-4 against KV8 ($+1.6$ [$-2.9$, $+6.0$]\pp{}). P3 rests on two blocks: the third lost one arm to a server timeout that recurred on a single-request re-run, and both admitted blocks depend on the re-run of arms lost to server failures (strict rule: not estimable). Net of the kernel path, the quantization effect is $+0.6$ [$-51.7$, $+52.9$] (KIVI-2) and $+2.4$ [$-5.4$, $+10.1$]\pp{} (KIVI-4), and the unquantized kernel path itself reproduces B's action in 85.1\% of states ($D_{cc}=-1.2$ [$-6.2$, $+3.9$]). % src: analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.md; analysis/fork/out/newmethod_q8_20261004T051538Z.json (tau2.cells.*.all_states_pp; tau2.contrasts; fidelity.cells.*.KLu_mean; fidelity.across_cells_spearman_KLu_mean_vs_M_cc); docs/insights/NEWMETHOD_PANEL_PROTOCOL.md (A1.2, A4.4, A7, A7.E1, A7.E2); analysis/fork/kivi_gate/GATE.md (§6)

\paragraph{Model family: the same picture on Mistral.} On Mistral-Small-3.2-24B (a pre-registered 83-task population; GPTQ-W4 on six blocks, RTN-W4 on five), replacing Q's first divergent action with B's again flips outcomes in both directions without net value. In GPTQ-W4, B's action rescues $r=4.6$ [2.4, 6.8] and damages $d=5.4$ [2.4, 8.5]\pp{} of states ($M=10.0$ [7.0, 13.1]), and no mode's net value is resolved ($G=-0.8$ [$-5.2$, $+3.6$], $H=+1.4$ [$-3.0$, $+5.8$], $F=+0.0$ [$-4.2$, $+4.2$]). RTN-W4 is similar ($M=10.4$ [6.3, 14.4]). The pre-registered contrast GPTQ $-$ RTN is not resolved: $\Delta D_{cc}=+2.2$ [$-3.5$, $+7.8$]\pp{}, $p=.35$, on five blocks. Under the strict rule, only three blocks remain ($+1.2$ [$-13.8$, $+16.2$]), because two blocks are admitted only by a rule adopted after an interim look; that rule scores the agent's own invalid tool calls as failures.
