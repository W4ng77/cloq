\paragraph{What is established.} Without a simulated user (BFCL, ALFWorld) and on the pre-registered telecom holdout, the replacement rescues and damages and its net value is small or negative; at a larger scale (Qwen3-14B, BFCL) value can appear in the action, and at a lower bit width (q8 RTN-W3) it can move to the continuation (\S\ref{sec:value}). \paragraph{Cache location: does KV-cache quantization move value to the continuation?} KIVI \citep{liu2024kivi} quantizes keys per channel and values per token. Our implementation is a replay-compatible variant: at every decode step it reconstructs the quantized view that official KIVI's token-by-token decode would hold at that position from the full-precision cache, rather than persisting a compressed cache across turns, so every token, prompt or generated, attends with that view.
