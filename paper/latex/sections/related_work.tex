\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Quantized agents and flat scores.} \emph{Flat Score, Amplified Failures} \citep{jang2026flat} is the closest study in setting. It compares 16-, 8- and 4-bit agents on \tautwo{} retail and telecom. Scores stay nearly flat while process errors rise, a tolerant error budget masks the damage, and the failure set is nearly the same at every precision (rank correlation $\ge 0.94$). % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (row 2)
That study compares whole episodes run independently, and its counterfactual re-scores episodes under a smaller error budget. We instead branch from identical states, which separates the changed action from what follows. We also measure signed outcome flips, rescues as well as damages, which an error-volume analysis does not show. Our ``fragile tasks shared across quantizers'' (\S\ref{sec:atlas}) overlaps with its stable failure set. What we add there is that the \emph{direction} of a shared flip is close to a coin toss across quantizers. Two-sided flips under compression were first emphasised on static tasks \citep{dutta2024accuracy}. Aggregate agent-compression benchmarks report scores \citep{dong2025acbench}. Precision sensitivity is localised to steps \citep{li2026dynamicmp}, phases \citep{lu2026mixquant} or task types \citep{zhang2026quantclaw}. DMR \citep{li2026dynamicmp} learns per-step BF16/W4 routing from KL supervision and episode reward. Our $G$ is the outcome counterfactual that such a router would harvest at one decision; we measure it rather than learn a proxy for it. \citet{lotfi2026quantized} give a token-level mechanism for quantized reasoning models. High-KL positions coincide with high entropy, and there the quantized model over-samples markers such as ``wait''. A logit penalty on those markers both repairs the damage and validates the mechanism. Our analogous token-level diagnosis at shared states is pending (\S\ref{sec:banku}).

\paragraph{Counterfactual replay and forking.} Single-step action swaps that let the same policy continue are now common. They are used for training \citep{li2026cso,li2026crr}, for attribution \citep{shah2026car} and for locating the decisive error \citep{zhang2025agentracer}. Each fills one cell of our design: an alternative action under a fixed continuation. \emph{The Replay Gap} \citep{gonuguntla2026replay} forks live SWE-agent trajectories and swaps the \emph{continuation} model, comparing Qwen3-4B (FP8) with Qwen3-14B (AWQ), against same-model control forks. Its 359 control forks had 0 success-label flips. Its FP8-served controls diverged in action sequence on 90--96\% of forks. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (row 1); docs/REPORT_GPT61_20261001_0915.md (correction 3)
We do not claim a lower outcome noise floor than that work. The difference is the design. We fork at a precision-induced divergence between two precisions of the same weights. We cross the action with the continuation controller. We also require every no-op replay to be bit-exact in prompt tokens, messages and database hashes (\S\ref{sec:replay}). None of the 32 closest works we checked crosses both axes or defines $r,d,M,G$ as estimands. Novelty therefore rests on the factorial design and the shared-state results, not on forking itself.

\paragraph{Evaluation noise and user simulators.} Serving numerics change greedy trajectories. In multi-turn tool use, prefix caching changed the trajectory in 36.2\% of episodes at 16-bit and 75.0\% at 4-bit; with the cache off, runs were bit-identical \citep{patodiya2026same}. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (row 16)
Agentic evaluations also vary from run to run \citep{bjarnason2026randomness,chen2026noisefloor}. The user simulator matters too. Changing the user model moves $\tau$-bench retail success by up to 9 points \citep{seshadri2026lost} and reward by 15.2 points \citep{jain2026userproxybench}. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (rows 23-24)
Because most of our consequential differences are text, the simulator is a plausible mediator. A second-simulator control is pending (\S\ref{sec:banku}).
