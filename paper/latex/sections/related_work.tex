\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Quantized agents compared episode by episode.} \emph{Flat Score, Amplified Failures} \citep{jang2026flat} is closest in setting: on \tautwo{} retail and telecom, 4-bit agents keep a flat task score while process damage grows, paired tasks flip in both directions, and recovery is equally frequent at both precisions. % src: docs/insights/FLATSCORE_COMPARISON.md
Its episodes are sampled independently. The failing action and the recovering controller therefore change precision together. We vary them separately at a fixed state. Two-sided flips under compression were first shown on static tasks \citep{dutta2024accuracy}; compression benchmarks compare tasks and models \citep{dong2025acbench}.

\paragraph{Forks and counterfactual replay.} \emph{The Replay Gap} \citep{gonuguntla2026replay} forks live SWE-bench Verified trajectories for per-step model switching. Its swap replaces the next action and continuation together, corresponding to our joint intervention. Its control holds the model fixed; ours holds the bit width fixed. % src: outputs/.drafts/replay_gap_2608.08239_excerpt.md; outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (row 1)
Replacing one action at a restored state and letting the original policy continue (our ``this step only'' contrast) locates decisive errors \citep{zhang2025agentracer}, attributes failures \citep{shah2026car} and yields training signal \citep{li2026cso,li2026crr}; the factorial design itself is standard. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (TL;DR, findings (b))

\paragraph{Precision routing, escalation and their signals.} DMR \citep{li2026dynamicmp} learns a per-step BF16/W4 router from KL supervision and episode reward; sensitivity has been localised to phases \citep{lu2026mixquant}, task types \citep{zhang2026quantclaw} and high-entropy, high-KL tokens \citep{lotfi2026quantized}. Agents also defer single decisions when uncertain \citep{piatrashyn2026redact}, learn cost-aware escalation \citep{lei2026tacit}, or are routed per step against execution-verified labels \citep{yang2026twinrouterbench}. Continuing another model's trajectory has its own cost \citep{ganz2026handoff}. Confidence does not reliably locate consequential steps under executed replay \citep{zhang2026credit}. KL measures disagreement magnitude without identifying its direction \citep{nikolic2026displacement}. Our evaluation measures action-only, continuation-only and joint substitutions on shared states, using rescue--damage accounting and reference interventions to interpret their task value.

\paragraph{Evaluation noise and user simulators.} Prefix caching changed multi-turn tool-use trajectories in 36.2\% of episodes at 16-bit and 75.0\% at 4-bit \citep{patodiya2026same}; agentic evaluations vary from run to run \citep{bjarnason2026randomness,chen2026noisefloor} and with the user model \citep{seshadri2026lost,jain2026userproxybench}. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (row 16)
