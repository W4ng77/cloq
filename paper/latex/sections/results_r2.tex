A large turnover or a frequent interaction is tempting evidence that a state needs higher precision. The same-precision substitution (\S\ref{sec:estimands}) tests this reading: if these consequences required a higher bit width, swapping one checkpoint for another at the same bit width should produce markedly fewer of them. It also benchmarks B's substitution against a fixed-precision alternative. Because each design forks at its own first divergence, the two may select different states, so shared and exclusive flips cannot be split into effects caused and not caused by precision.

\paragraph{A higher bit width is not necessary for two-sided flips and continuation dependence.} They also appear when only the calibration checkpoint is swapped (Figure~\ref{fig:sameprec}a). At W4 (interim, \pending{6} blocks), the second checkpoint's action rescues $r'=9.3$ [7.0, 11.7] and damages $d'=8.4$ [6.4, 10.5]\pp{} of units ($M'=17.7$\% against $M=16.8$\% for B), and 26.2\% of its divergent states are off the diagonal, against 25.0\% for B; each same-precision rate has a 95\% lower bound above zero. % src: docs/insights/SAMEPREC_PROTOCOL.md (A1.3, 95% lower bound); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_20261002T142834Z.md (TL;DR; Full pool; On divergent states; analysis set K = 6)
In the W3 stress pair the turnovers are 17.3 and 18.8\pp{} and the off-diagonal shares 27.6\% and 27.3\%. Neither pair shows the two turnovers to be equivalent within $\pm$3\pp{} (Appendix~\ref{app:sameprec}), so flip rate and interaction size alone cannot diagnose a need for a higher bit width. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_20261003T023933Z.md (TL;DR; Full pool, pool rows)

\paragraph{Units affected by both, or by only one, substitution.} Figure~\ref{fig:sameprec}b compares the affected units. At W4, 107 units flip under both substitutions, 64 only under B and 74 only under the second checkpoint. The reference that matters keeps each task's flip propensity: a within-task block shuffle gives 76 [69, 84] units flipping under both, so the substitutions share affected units beyond the fact that the same tasks are flip-prone anyway. Assuming instead that the two flip sets are independent within cell and block would give 30. At W3, the counts are 62, 34 and 26. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_overlap_20261002T143218Z.md (Overlap table; Agreement); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_split_20261003T024007Z.md (Figure-panel table, G rows)
The shared core is on the action side: at W4, B's and the second checkpoint's rescue sets agree with $\kappa=0.60$ and their harm sets with $\kappa=0.54$. When B also takes over the continuation, the affected units are largely substitution-specific: rescue sets under the continuation-only contrast agree with $\kappa=0.20$, and 70\% of continuation-dependent units are continuation-dependent under one substitution only. The difference in net value comes only from the substitution-specific units and is not resolved ($-1.6$ [$-6.2$, $+3.0$]\pp{}). These analyses are descriptive (Appendix~\ref{app:sameprec}, Figure~\ref{fig:split}). % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_split_20261002T145148Z.md (Figure-panel table; §1; §2; §3)

\paragraph{Common states.} Each design above selects its own divergent states. A pre-registered comparison holds the states fixed: on 510 ordinary q8 states (85 tasks $\times$ 6 blocks), the current action comes from B, from Q or from the second checkpoint Q$'$, and B or Q continues; $K(c)=V(\mathrm{B},c)-V(\mathrm{Q}',c)$ asks what B's action adds beyond another W4 action under continuation $c$. At the confirmatory second look, $K=+1.0$\pp{} under Q's continuation and $+0.8$\pp{} under B's (Figure~\ref{fig:commonstate}). Neither a difference nor equivalence within $\pm$3\pp{} is shown at the two-look level, so the comparison is \emph{not resolved}; under B's continuation the upper 97\% bounds put any extra value of B's action at no more than $+3.8$\pp{} ($t$) and $+3.7$\pp{} (bootstrap). It does not show that B's action lacks extra value. Details, the first look and the secondary interaction are in Appendix~\ref{app:sameprec}. % src: docs/insights/COMMONSTATE_SOURCES_PROTOCOL.md (§2, §5, §6; A1.2, A1.3; D2.2); analysis/fork/out/commonstate_q8ext_20261003T231159Z.md (TL;DR; Two-look reading table)
B's own measured values stand: on 20 blocks its action is worth $\Gall=+2.9$\pp{} in q8 and $-1.9$\pp{} in q4 GPTQ-W4 (\S\ref{sec:value}); the reference changes how they are read, not what they are. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md
