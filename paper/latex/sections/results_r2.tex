\section{Sensitivity Without Higher Precision}
\label{sec:sameprec}

A large turnover or a frequent interaction is tempting evidence that a state needs higher precision. If so, the same-precision reference substitution (\S\ref{sec:estimands}), which swaps one W4 checkpoint for another, should produce markedly fewer of these consequences; we also ask whether the two substitutions rescue and harm the same units.

\paragraph{A higher bit width is not necessary for two-sided flips and continuation dependence.} They also appear when only the W4 calibration checkpoint is swapped (Figure~\ref{fig:sameprec}a). On the first \pending{6} complete blocks (interim), Q$'$ diverges from Q as often as B does (76.9\% of units in both designs). Its action rescues $r'=9.3$ [7.0, 11.7] and damages $d'=8.4$ [6.4, 10.5]\pp{} of units ($M'=17.7$\%, against $M=16.8$\% for B), and 26.2\% [20.7, 31.3] of its divergent states are off the diagonal, against 25.0\% for B. Every one of these same-precision rates has a 95\% lower bound above zero. % src: docs/insights/SAMEPREC_PROTOCOL.md (A1.3, 95% lower bound); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_20261002T142834Z.md (TL;DR; Full pool; On divergent states; analysis set K = 6)
The difference in turnover is $\Delta M=M-M'=-0.98$\pp{} (90\% CI [$-3.26$, $+1.30]$). No precision-specific excess was detected, but equivalence within $\pm$3\pp{} is not shown either (two one-sided tests, $p=0.067$), and the same holds for the net gain ($\Delta\Gall=-1.57$ [$-5.17$, $+2.04$], $p=0.230$) and, descriptively, for the off-diagonal share ($-1.210$ [$-4.09$, $+1.67$], $p=0.133$, as the mean of per-block ratios, which the interval and test use; $-1.216$ as the ratio of block sums; Figure~\ref{fig:sameprec}b). % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_20261002T142834Z.md (Amendment A1 table, pool rows); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_20261003T023857Z.json (tost_a1.consistency.d_offdiag: -1.21 stored at 3-decimal rounding); derived: 1.210=1.21
We do not say that the designs are equivalent, only that flip rate and interaction size alone cannot diagnose a need for an upgrade: a fixed-precision swap already produces them at comparable size.

\paragraph{Similar flip volume, overlapping but not identical units.} Of 1020 units, 107 flip under both substitutions, against 30 expected if the two flip sets were independent within cell and block (Cohen's $\kappa=0.53$ [0.46, 0.58]; Figure~\ref{fig:sameprec}c). Sharing goes beyond task difficulty: a within-task block shuffle, which keeps every task's flip propensity, gives only 76 [69, 84]. Yet 138 of the 245 flipping units flip under only one substitution. These overlap analyses are descriptive and were added after the interim result was seen. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_overlap_20261002T143218Z.md (Overlap table; Agreement; Task-level); derived: 138=64+74; 245=107+64+74
\paragraph{A shared action-side core, substitution-specific continuation effects.} Splitting each contrast into rescues and harms separates two regimes (Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:split}). On the action side ($G$), the two substitutions share a large core: 56 of B's 82 rescues and 51 of its 89 harms are also rescues and harms of Q$'$ ($\kappa=0.60$ and $0.54$), above a within-task shuffle (37 and 25 shared). Still, 56\% of the units that flip under either substitution flip under only one, against 72\% under the shuffle. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_split_20261002T145148Z.md (Figure-panel table; §1)
On the continuation side ($H$) and under full substitution ($F$), overlap is at or near the shuffle level ($H$ rescues $\kappa=0.20$; $F$ $\kappa=0.34$ and $0.35$, at chance under a shuffle within task and QQ outcome). Continuation dependence is largely substitution-specific: among the 697 units that diverge in both designs, agreement on being off the diagonal is $\kappa=0.29$ against 0.23 under the shuffle, and 70\% of off-diagonal units are off-diagonal under one design only. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_split_20261002T145148Z.md (§1; §2); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_split_20261002T145148Z.json (controller.both_divergent: kappa_null_mean 0.2291, share_specific_pct 69.55)
Under full substitution B flips more units than Q$'$ (193 vs.\ 149; $+4.3$ [$+0.4$, $+8.3$]\pp{}, one of several contrasts); the net difference comes only from substitution-specific units and is not resolved ($\Delta G=-1.6$ [$-6.2$, $+3.0$]\pp{}). Only value measures on the same units reveal this structure. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_split_20261002T145148Z.md (§3 net decomposition; cross-direction counts)

\paragraph{Stress check at W3.} The protocol's complete W3 panel (Q GPTQ-W3, Q$'$ a second W3 calibration draw; q4 and q8, $K=3$ blocks q3--q5, 510 units) is reported next to W4, never pooled with it. The same-precision substitution produces 17.3\pp{} of turnover, the BF16 substitution 18.8\pp{}; off-diagonal shares are 27.6\% and 27.3\%. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_20261003T023933Z.md (TL;DR; Full pool, pool rows); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_split_20261003T024007Z.md (510 units); docs/insights/SAMEPREC_W3_PROTOCOL_20261002.md (K = 3, q3-q5)
$\Delta M=+1.6$\pp{} (95\% [$-0.66$, $+3.80$]) is unresolved: the preregistered $\pm$3\pp{} equivalence test fails ($p=0.055$); that the 90\% interval lies just above zero does not change this. $\Delta\Gall$ is equivalent within $\pm$3\pp{} only in the preregistered pooled population ($+1.2$, $p=0.017$); per model it is $+3.1$ (q4) and $-0.8$\pp{} (q8). The off-diagonal difference is $-0.288$ (ratio of block sums) or $-0.307$ (mean of per-block ratios, used by the interval and test: 90\% [$-11.0$, $+10.4]$, $p=0.27$). % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_20261003T023933Z.md (Amendment A1 table; Paired differences); analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_20261003T023933Z.json (pooled.paired.dM.ci95; tost_a1.consistency.d_offdiag)
On the action side ($G$), rescued and harmed task$\times$block units overlap ($\kappa=0.60$, $0.68$); the designs may first diverge at different decisions, so this is unit overlap, not a same-state replication. Of 122 units whose $G$ outcome flips, 62 flip under both substitutions, 34 only under BF16 and 26 only under the other W3 checkpoint: 49\% respond to only one. Flip volume, affected units and net upgrade value must be measured separately. % src: analysis/fork/out/sameprec_w3_split_20261003T024007Z.md (Figure-panel table, G rows; TL;DR); derived: 122=62+34+26

\paragraph{The measured value of the BF16 replacement stands.} The reference changes how B's consequences are read, not what they are: on 20 blocks B's action is worth $\Gall=+2.9$\pp{} in q8 and $-1.9$\pp{} in q4 GPTQ-W4 (\S\ref{sec:value}). On the interim same-precision blocks, B's and Q$'$'s net gains ($-0.7$ [$-3.9$, $+2.5$] and $+0.9$ [$-3.1$, $+4.8$]\pp{}) are no conclusion about value; the next comparison asks it directly. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md; analysis/fork/out/sameprec_20261002T142834Z.md (Full pool, pool q4+q8 rows)

\paragraph{Common-state action sources.} Above, each design selects its own divergent states. A pre-registered comparison removes that difference: on 255 ordinary q8 states (85 tasks $\times$ 3 blocks, sampled before any outcome; none excluded), the current action comes from B, from Q (GPTQ-W4, first checkpoint) or from Q$'$ (second checkpoint), and B or Q continues. $K(c)=V(\mathrm{B},c)-V(\mathrm{Q}',c)$ asks what B's action adds beyond another W4 action. A frozen rule claims value only if both 95\% intervals exclude zero (Holm), equivalence only if both 90\% intervals lie within $\pm$3\pp{}. % src: docs/insights/COMMONSTATE_SOURCES_PROTOCOL.md (§2 design, §5 estimands, §6 frozen reading); analysis/fork/out/commonstate_20261002T201612Z.md (header; Excluded units: 0); derived: 255=85*3
B's action is not shown to be worth more than Q$'$'s (Figure~\ref{fig:commonstate}b): under Q's continuation $K=-0.4$\pp{} (95\%: $t$ [$-9.3$, $+8.5$], bootstrap [$-3.5$, $+2.7$]) and under B's $K=+2.4$\pp{} ([$-3.5$, $+8.2$], [$-0.8$, $+5.5$]). Both are ``not resolved'': neither a difference nor equivalence is shown. A second W4 checkpoint's action departs from Q's as often as B's does (agreement with Q's action 55.7\% against 57.6\%; B with Q$'$ 56.9\%; Figure~\ref{fig:commonstate}c). % src: analysis/fork/out/commonstate_20261002T201612Z.md (Key estimand table; Contrasts table, M column; Action agreement)
Any edge of B appears only when B also continues (31.4\% against 29.0\%; Figure~\ref{fig:commonstate}a), consistent with continuation dependence (\S\ref{sec:value}) but unresolved on three blocks (interaction $+2.7$\pp{}, 90\%: $t$ [$+0.5$, $+5.0$], bootstrap [$-0.4$, $+5.9$]; secondary). % src: analysis/fork/out/commonstate_20261002T201612Z.md (V table; Interaction table, bf16 − d2 row)
Changing outcomes is thus not improving them; this does not make precision irrelevant or the two actions equivalent.
