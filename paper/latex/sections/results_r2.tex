\paragraph{On average over W4 cells, no option has a detectable gain.} Pooled over the four W4 cells (Figure~\ref{fig:ghf}), $G=+0.5$, $H=+0.2$ and $F=+0.7$\pp{}, and every interval covers zero, as do their differences. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (pools)

\paragraph{Within cells, the paired gains differ.} The following contrasts are exploratory, and their intervals treat the 85 tasks as fixed. In q8 GPTQ-W4, $G=+3.3$ [$+1.6$, $+5.0$]\pp{} exceeds $H=+0.4$ ($G-H=+2.9$ [$+1.3$, $+4.7$]), and $F=+5.4$ exceeds $G$ ($F-G=+2.1$ [$+0.2$, $+4.0$]). In q8 AWQ-W4, $G-H=+3.4$ [$+1.5$, $+5.2$] and $F-G=-1.5$ [$-4.2$, $+0.8$]. In q4 GPTQ-W4, $G=-2.9$ and $G-H=-3.6$ [$-6.6$, $-0.6$]. In the stress cell q8 RTN-W3 (three blocks), $H=+15.3$ and $F=+18.4$ against $G=-0.4$ ($G-H=-15.7$ [$-27.5$, $-3.9$]). In q4 KV8, B continuing after Q's action lowers success ($H=-2.5$ [$-4.5$, $-0.5$], unadjusted). % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1; n_tasks)
After Holm adjustment over the four core W4 cells, $G-H$ stays below .05 in both q8 cells ($p=.013$, $.012$) but not in q4 GPTQ-W4 ($p=.069$). % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_multiplicity_20261001T225727Z.md
For new tasks, the $G-H$ intervals of all four W4 cells cover zero (Table~\ref{tab:ghf-crossed}), and a smaller second-simulator control neither confirms nor reverses them (\S\ref{sec:r3}). Cells select different states, so orderings across cells cannot be attributed to the quantizer or bit width alone.

\paragraph{A BF16 reference action can lower success.} In q4 GPTQ-W4, B's action lowers success under Q's continuation on the fixed task pool ($G=-2.9$ [$-5.7$, $-0.2$]\pp{}, unadjusted; the new-task interval covers zero), with a similar point estimate under B's ($\DB=-3.2$ [$-6.4$, $+0.2$]). Here the reference action is not reliably better. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1, F - H)

\paragraph{The action's consequence often depends on who continues.} In 26.9\% [25.7, 28.1] of pooled W4 states, the action swap has a different effect under B's continuation than under Q's (Figure~\ref{fig:ctrl}; pooled $(\DQ,\DB)$ tables in Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:3x3}). Per W4 cell the share is 22.9--30.5\%, and in the KV8 pool it is 15.3\% [13.2, 17.3]. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§2)
Because the setting is deterministic and replay-verified, each such state shows a non-additive interaction on the success scale; on its own this does not establish a recovery or quantization-specific mechanism, or a deployment gain. Cohen's $\kappa$ between $\DQ$ and $\DB$, against a marginal-preserving re-pairing of states, is 0.29 in the W4 pool; it measures how well the two effects agree across states, not whether the interaction is significant (Appendix~\ref{app:agreement}). % src: analysis/fork/out/agreement_control_20261002T024240Z.md (§2 equal-weight pools W4:all)
Strict sign reversals are rare: 3.2\% of pooled W4 states, and 1.2--4.2\% of states in each W4 and KV8 cell, or 8--14\% of the off-diagonal mass. The rest is an effect under one controller and none under the other. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (TL;DR; §2)

\paragraph{Controller dependence without an LLM user.} In a post hoc analysis of BFCL multi-turn and ALFWorld, which have no simulated user (one run per item; Figure~\ref{fig:ctrl}, Table~\ref{tab:extr2}), the off-diagonal share is 4.7--17.1\% in the four BFCL W4 cells (pool 10.3\%) and 32.8--40.0\% in the two q8 ALFWorld W4 cells (pool 36.4\%). Controller dependence therefore does not require an LLM user simulator, and its size differs by environment. % src: analysis/fork/out/ext_r2_20261002T024433Z.md (§3, §6)

\paragraph{Why the mean interaction is near zero.} $E[I]$ is the difference between the swap's average effects under the two controllers, whereas the off-diagonal share is $P(I\neq0)$. In the W4 pool, states where the swap helps more under Q ($I>0$) and under B ($I<0$) are about equally frequent (13.6\% and 13.4\%), so $E[I]=+0.0$ [$-1.9$, $+2.0$]\pp{} although 26.9\% of states depend on the controller (new-task interval 22.1--32.0\%). % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§2, pool W4:all; crossed column)
The value of a fixed replacement policy is $E[G]$, $E[H]$ or $E[F]$, not $E[I]$. All 16 outcome patterns are in Appendix~\ref{app:patterns}.

\paragraph{Same-precision baseline.} To separate precision-specific effects from generic trajectory sensitivity, we repeat the fork with Q$'$, a second GPTQ-W4 calibration draw of the same model, in place of B. Everything else is unchanged: q4 and q8 GPTQ-W4, the same Q episodes and task panel, and a protocol frozen before any outcome existed.
