\paragraph{On average over W4 cells, no option has a detectable gain.} Pooled over the four W4 cells (Figure~\ref{fig:ghf}), $G=+0.5$, $H=+0.2$ and $F=+0.7$\pp{}, and every interval covers zero, as do their differences. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (pools)

\paragraph{Within cells, the paired gains differ.} In q8 GPTQ-W4, $G=+3.3$ [$+1.6$, $+5.0$]\pp{} exceeds $H=+0.4$ ($G-H=+2.9$ [$+1.3$, $+4.7$]), and $F=+5.4$ exceeds $G$ ($F-G=+2.1$ [$+0.2$, $+4.0$]). In q8 AWQ-W4, $G-H=+3.4$ [$+1.5$, $+5.2$] and $F-G=-1.5$ [$-4.2$, $+0.8$]. In q4 GPTQ-W4, $G=-2.9$ and $G-H=-3.6$ [$-6.6$, $-0.6$]. In the stress cell q8 RTN-W3 (three blocks, exploratory), $H=+15.3$ and $F=+18.4$ against $G=-0.4$ ($G-H=-15.7$ [$-27.5$, $-3.9$]). In q4 KV8, B continuing after Q's action lowers success: $H=-2.5$ [$-4.5$, $-0.5$] (fixed pool, unadjusted; exploratory). % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1)
These intervals treat the 85 tasks as fixed. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (n_tasks)
Holm-adjusted over the four core W4 cells, $G-H$ remains below .05 in both q8 cells ($p=.013$, $.012$) but not in q4 GPTQ-W4 ($p=.069$), % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_multiplicity_20261001T225727Z.md
and for new tasks the $G-H$ intervals of all four W4 cells cover zero (Table~\ref{tab:ghf-crossed}). We therefore treat the $G-H$ results as exploratory; a smaller second-simulator control does not confirm them (\S\ref{sec:r3}). Cells select different states, so orderings across cells are descriptive and cannot be attributed to the quantizer or bit width alone.

\paragraph{A BF16 reference action can lower success.} In q4 GPTQ-W4, B's action lowers success under Q's continuation on the fixed task pool ($G=-2.9$ [$-5.7$, $-0.2$]\pp{}, unadjusted; new-task interval covers zero), with a similar point estimate under B's ($\DB=-3.2$ [$-6.4$, $+0.2$]); the reference action itself is not reliably better here. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§1, F - H)

\paragraph{The action's consequence often depends on who continues.} Figure~\ref{fig:ctrl} shows the per-cell shares; the pooled $(\DQ,\DB)$ tables are in Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:3x3}. In 26.9\% [25.7, 28.1] of pooled W4 states, the action swap has a different effect under B's continuation than under Q's; per W4 cell the share is 22.9--30.5\%. In the KV8 pool it is 15.3\% [13.2, 17.3]; the W4-KV8 difference is descriptive. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§2)
A marginal-preserving pairing agreement control, which re-pairs states at random within a cell, expects 38.0\% [36.7, 39.0] in the W4 pool ($\kappa=0.29$ [0.27, 0.32], new-task [0.22, 0.36]) and 33.6\% in KV8 ($\kappa=0.55$; Appendix~\ref{app:agreement}): the effects under the two controllers agree more than random cross-state pairing would. % src: analysis/fork/out/agreement_control_20261002T024240Z.md (§2 equal-weight pools W4:all, KV8:all)
This control is not a no-interaction null. At the same state, in our deterministic, replay-verified setting, $I\neq0$ is a non-additive interaction on the success scale, whose identification does not depend on exceeding the control; it does not by itself establish a recovery or quantization-specific mechanism, or a deployment gain.
Strict sign reversals are rare: 3.2\% of pooled W4 states, and 1.2--4.2\% of states in each W4 and KV8 cell. They make up 8--14\% of the off-diagonal mass. The rest is an effect under one controller and none under the other. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (TL;DR; §2)

\paragraph{Controller dependence without an LLM user.} BFCL multi-turn and ALFWorld have no simulated user (post hoc, exploratory; one run per item; Figure~\ref{fig:ctrl}, Table~\ref{tab:extr2}). The off-diagonal share is 4.7--17.1\% in the four BFCL W4 cells (pool 10.3\%) and 32.8--40.0\% in the two q8 ALFWorld W4 cells (pool 36.4\%). As in \tautwo{}, the shares lie below the marginal-preserving pairing agreement control (expected 16.2--32.4\% and 42.9--46.4\%; $\kappa$ 0.47--0.71 and 0.14--0.24). Controller dependence therefore also occurs without an LLM user simulator; its size differs by environment (descriptive). % src: analysis/fork/out/ext_r2_20261002T024433Z.md (§3, §6)

\paragraph{Why the mean interaction is near zero.} $E[I]$ is the difference between the swap's average effect under the two controllers, whereas the off-diagonal share is $P(I\neq0)$. States where the swap helps more under Q ($I>0$) and under B ($I<0$) are about equally frequent (13.6\% and 13.4\% in the W4 pool), so $E[I]=+0.0$ [$-1.9$, $+2.0$]\pp{} although 26.9\% of states depend on the controller (new-task interval 22.1--32.0\%). % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§2, pool W4:all; crossed column)
The value of a fixed replacement policy is $E[G]$, $E[H]$ or $E[F]$, not $E[I]$. All 16 outcome patterns: Appendix~\ref{app:patterns}.
