\paragraph{Selecting outcome-changing decisions.} $G$, $H$ and $F$ require running B. We test selection under a Q-only constraint: the rule uses no BF16 computation before choosing an upgrade. Development diagnostics link uncertainty to action disagreement more clearly than to its consequences (Appendix~\ref{app:token}); \S\ref{sec:discussion} examines where this gap enters the fidelity--value relationship. % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261001T185031Z.md (§1a; §1b differing vs exact)
On one development block, we choose among entropy, margin and mean log-probability by net gain over random selection. We freeze the winning margin rule before validation: at low minimum token margin, B supplies the action and Q continues. This implements $G$; margins are known to predict cross-precision divergence \citep{du2026greedy}. Per-configuration thresholds target 5, 10 and 20\% of development states. Validation can select a different fraction, so random and oracle baselines match the actual counts (Figure~\ref{fig:trigger}). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261002T003130Z.md; manifests/fork/token_trigger_rule_v1.json

We test on two later blocks of the same development tasks, separate from telecom holdout. The states come from Q's own histories. Six configurations (four W4 and two KV8) yield 1020 states, including 69 outcome changes. Capture exceeds matched random selection at 5 and 20\% (one-sided $p=.0182$ and $.0002$); at 10\%, $p=.0613$. The secondary test on BF16 histories enriches changes only at 20\% (Appendix~\ref{app:token}). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261002T003130Z.md (§3 Validation, U1 S_mm PRIMARY; U2B S_mm secondary); manifests/fork/token_trigger_rule_v1.json (population; candidates; selection_criterion)

\paragraph{Testing benefit against random selection.} The captured states were rescues and damages alike (Figure~\ref{fig:trigger}a). None of the three rates shows a reliable net benefit over matched random selection after Holm correction (Figure~\ref{fig:trigger}b). No rate was designated primary; exact tests and the nominal 5\% result appear in Appendix~\ref{app:oracle}. A hindsight oracle with the same per-configuration counts nets far more (Figure~\ref{fig:trigger}b), positive by construction because it picks only rescues. A post hoc KL$(\mathrm{B}\|\mathrm{Q})$ score, which needs BF16, also captures turnover above chance on these states without a significant net gain (exploratory, post hoc; Appendices~\ref{app:oracle} and~\ref{app:kl}). % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261002T003130Z.md (§3 Validation, U1 S_mm PRIMARY); analysis/fork/out/oracle_trigger_20261002T024423Z.md (§1a, §2)

\paragraph{What this test establishes.} Enriching outcome-changing decisions is not enough for reliable net gain \citep[cf.][]{zhang2026credit}. The later blocks reuse the development tasks, and only this simple frozen rule is tested. Learned and other uncertainty-based escalation rules require their own net-benefit tests \citep{li2026dynamicmp,piatrashyn2026redact,lei2026tacit}.
