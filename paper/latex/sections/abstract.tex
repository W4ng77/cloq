\begin{abstract}
In a closed-loop agent, the value of a single precision upgrade depends on the action it changes and on the execution that follows. When a quantized agent and its BF16 reference act differently at a state, should BF16 supply this step, take over from the next step, or both? We measure this with a crossed same-state intervention. At a frozen state that replays bit-exactly, we cross the source of the current action (BF16 or quantized) with the controller of the rest of the episode, a standard $2\times2$ factorial design made executable for agents. We study Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B under simulated weight and KV-cache quantization with BF16 compute, using one greedy user simulator in \tautwo{} and forking at the first decision where the two models' actions differ in tool structure. % nocheck: model sizes and design label
We report three findings. (i)~The value of an action depends on the controller that continues. In 26.9\% of development W4 divergent states, the same action swap has a different outcome effect under BF16 and under quantized continuation, and this dependence also appears on BFCL and ALFWorld, which have no LLM user. A same-precision baseline, with a second GPTQ-W4 calibration draw in place of BF16, tests how much of it is specific to precision: \pending{[same-precision reading pending]}. % src: analysis/fork/out/ghf_20261001T211519Z.md (§2); analysis/fork/out/ext_r2_20261002T024433Z.md (§3, §6); docs/insights/SAMEPREC_PROTOCOL.md
(ii)~Local upgrades redistribute success and failure. Replacing a W4 agent's action rescues or damages 15.5--20.9\% of task-block units while the net effect stays small; on unseen telecom tasks the estimated effect is $-4.6$\pp{} (90\% CI $-7.2$ to $-2.0$), which the pre-registered readings classify as inconclusive. % src: analysis/fork/out/dev_all_20261001T070326Z.md; analysis/fork/out/holdout_confirm_20261002T012353Z.md
(iii)~Finding sensitive decisions and choosing beneficial replacements are different targets. A frozen uncertainty trigger captures more outcome-changing decisions than matched random selection at two of three rates, but has not been shown to yield a net gain. % src: analysis/fork/out/token_mech_20261002T003130Z.md (§3)
Precision upgrades for agents should be judged by the task value of the replacement under the execution that follows it.
\end{abstract}
