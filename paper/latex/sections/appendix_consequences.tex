\subsection{Outcome Classes and a Label-Permutation Reference} % nocheck: heading
\label{app:offdiag}

Everything in this section is exploratory and post hoc. It uses the four outcomes of each divergent state in the order BB, BQ, QB, QQ; $k$ is the number of successful arms.

\paragraph{Five classes.} The 16 patterns fall into five classes: unchanged (\texttt{0000}, \texttt{1111}); action only (\texttt{1100}, \texttt{0011}: the outcome follows the action under both continuations); continuation only (\texttt{1010}, \texttt{0101}); one arm differs from the other three ($k$ odd); strict reversal (\texttt{1001}, \texttt{0110}). % nocheck: pattern labels
Changing the action changes the outcome under at least one continuation exactly in the action-only, one-arm and reversal classes, and changing the continuation exactly in the continuation-only, one-arm and reversal classes. Both change it exactly on the off-diagonal states, so the two shares overlap and must not be added, and their gap equals action only minus continuation only: it comes from $k=2$ states alone and restates the $k=2$ arrangement below on all divergent states. Two named patterns follow the upgrade modes: joint rescue (\texttt{1000}: only the joint upgrade succeeds) and rescue undone ($\YY{BQ}=1$, $\YY{QQ}=0$, $\YY{BB}=0$: B's action rescues under Q's continuation and B's takeover of the continuation undoes it). % nocheck: definitions
Table~\ref{tab:classes} gives the shares. The action-side gap holds in the \tautwo{} retail/airline W4 and KV8 pools, in the W4 same-precision fork and in the BFCL W4 pool. It is near zero on the telecom development tasks ($+0.1$ [$-2.1$, $+2.4$]\pp{}, 6-block $t$) and in the q4 GPTQ-W3 and KV4-rot stress cells, and it is not resolved in the ALFWorld W4 pool ($+3.4$ [$-1.8$, $+8.4$]) or the W3 same-precision pair ($+1.9$ [$-2.9$, $+6.7$]). Its point estimate is negative in the q8 RTN-W3 stress cell ($-7.8$; 3-block $t$ [$-18.1$, $+2.4$], new-task [$-15.3$, $-1.2$]) and, not shown, in ALFWorld q8 GPTQ-W3 ($-19.2$ [$-27.3$, $-10.8$]). In the ALFWorld and BFCL KV4 cells (not shown) Q almost never succeeds, so continuation-only states dominate there mechanically. % src: analysis/fork/out/outcome_classes_20261004T143614Z.md

\paragraph{A label-permutation reference.} Given $k$, assigning the four outcomes to the four arms uniformly at random gives the off-diagonal share $E_0=P(k\ \mathrm{odd})+P(k=2)/3$. In every cell the observed share minus $E_0$ equals $P(k=2)\,[P(\mathrm{reversal}\mid k=2)-1/3]$, so it can depart from $E_0$ only through strict reversals. In the W4 pool the share is 26.9\% against $E_0=28.4\%$ ($-1.4$ [$-1.8$, $-1.0$]\pp{}, fixed pool, block bootstrap over 20 blocks; KV8: 15.3 against 18.9\%), because reversals are 23.6\% [19.2, 28.3] (task bootstrap) of $k=2$ states instead of one third. % src: analysis/fork/out/offdiag_null_20261004T030949Z.md
This shows only that strict reversals are rarer than under random labelling. It does not show that the continuation controller is irrelevant, which would require $\YY{BB}=\YY{BQ}$ and $\YY{QB}=\YY{QQ}$, or that the interaction is zero: the reference itself allows $I\neq0$ in most arrangements. Conversely, an off-diagonal state does not show a distinct interaction mechanism: if an arm succeeds when an action score plus a continuation score exceeds a threshold, exactly additive scores can make only BB succeed (\texttt{1000}), although they never produce a strict reversal. % nocheck: argument

\paragraph{Joint rescue and rescue undone against the reference.} The same reference, applied to the two named patterns, keeps $k$ for every state and permutes the four outcome labels uniformly: \texttt{1000} then has probability $1/4$ at $k=1$, and rescue undone (\texttt{0100} or \texttt{0110}) has $1/4$ at $k=1$ plus $1/6$ at $k=2$, so the reference shares are $P(k{=}1)/4$ and $P(k{=}1)/4+P(k{=}2)/6$, computed on the same states and bootstrap draws as the observed shares (Table~\ref{tab:patternref}). In the \tautwo{} W4 pool, joint rescue exceeds the reference by $+0.47$\pp{} on the fixed task pool ([$+0.06$, $+0.87$]), but the new-task interval spans zero ([$-0.33$, $+1.37$]); rescue undone is less frequent than the reference ($-0.99$ [$-1.58$, $-0.42]$; new tasks [$-2.19$, $+0.27$]). The excess of joint rescue is concentrated in the q8 cells, and no group shows a new-task excess of rescue undone. Because the reference keeps $k$ and does not model execution, a share near it says that the arrangement is about as common as a random arrangement of the same number of successes, not that the outcomes are noise or that the continuation is irrelevant. % src: analysis/fork/out/pattern_ref_20261005T134721Z.md (TL;DR; §1; §2; Method)

\subsection{Transfer of gain labels between upgrade modes}
\label{app:transfer}
\paragraph{Design.} Protocol \texttt{LABEL\_TRANSFER\_POSTHOC.md} (exploratory, post hoc: these outcomes had been seen). For a selection label $X$ and an executed mode $Y$, both in $\{G,H,F\}$, and a budget of 5, 10 or 20\%, we select by $X$ and report the executed rescues, damages and net of $Y$, with ties broken uniformly at random (exact expectation). In the split-half variant, tasks are ranked by mean $X$ on one half of the blocks (outcome-independent hash) and evaluated on the other half, averaged over both directions; the diagonal is a self-label reference, not an upper bound, since it can also do poorly on the evaluation half. Same-draw selection on one set of outcomes is a hindsight diagnostic: its loss is non-negative by construction, so only its gain over random selection is informative. Intervals are block and crossed task$\times$block bootstrap, uncorrected for the 18 off-diagonal comparisons per frame. Budgets count states or tasks, not BF16 compute: $H$ and $F$ use several BF16 decisions per selected state. % src: docs/insights/LABEL_TRANSFER_POSTHOC.md; analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md
\paragraph{Results.} The protocol designates ordinary states (Bank U U1, 3 blocks) as the primary frame and first-divergence states (Bank P) as secondary, and designates no budget or label pair. In the primary frame no split-half loss is resolved ($H\!\to\!F$ at 10\%: $+0.30$ [$-0.60$, $+1.11$]), and the self-label references themselves cover zero. In the secondary frame (W4 pool, split-half, pp of all units), at a 10\% task budget, the losses with crossed intervals above zero are $H\!\to\!F$ 2.82 [1.40, 3.90], $H\!\to\!G$ 2.43 [0.97, 3.68] and $G\!\to\!F$ 1.50 [0.20, 2.19]; at 5\%, $H\!\to\!G$ and $H\!\to\!F$ (1.70 each) also exclude zero, and at 20\%, $H\!\to\!F$ 3.39 [1.94, 4.91]. The mode ranking implied by the selection label matches the self-label ranking for $F$ at 10 and 20\% and for $G$ at 5\% (point estimates). Same-draw results, U2B and KV8 pools are in the output file. % src: analysis/fork/out/label_transfer_20261005T185537Z.md (Bank P split-half 5/10/20%; Bank U U1 split-half 10%)
