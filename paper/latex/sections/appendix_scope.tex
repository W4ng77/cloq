\subsection{Ordinary states and the user simulator}
\label{app:ordinary}

\paragraph{Ordinary states include agreements.} At ordinary states, replacing Q's action changes the outcome less often than at first tool-structure divergences (a descriptive comparison of different state populations, with no interval on the difference). Over blocks f01--f03 (task-equal; $t$ intervals over block means), $M$ per sub-bank is 7.8--9.0\% for the q4 W4 cells in U1 and U2B, 4.7--9.0\% for the q8 W4 cells and at most 4.7\% for KV8. The net gain lies within $\pm$2.8\pp{} (U1, U2B) and $\pm$5.0\pp{} (U2Q), and every interval covers zero (Appendix~\ref{app:banku}). % src: analysis/fork/out/bank_u_f01_f02_f03_20261002T003037Z.md (§2)
Token-identical actions preserve the outcome under deterministic serving. Reused arms were initially selected by canonical equality and then checked with each model's chat template: 4{,}113 of 4{,}114 reuses are token-identical. The remaining pair differs only in JSON key order; its two cross arms were measured separately and reproduced the reused outcomes. % src: analysis/fork/reuse_audit/REUSE_AUDIT.md (TL;DR); analysis/fork/reuse_audit/REUSE_FIX_RESULT.md (TL;DR)
\paragraph{Sensitivity to the user simulator.} Text actions act through the user simulator, and changing the simulator moves $\tau$-bench outcomes by 9--15 points \citep{seshadri2026lost,jain2026userproxybench}. % src: outputs/.drafts/storyline-v7-lit.md (rows 23-24)
We froze a control before running it: the 39 deduplicated text-difference flips with the same tool structure of block f01 and 39 matched non-flip states. Their BQ and QQ branches were each re-run three times with a different user simulator, Sonnet 5.5, answering through a file relay from Claude Code subagents (reproducibility limits in the Limitations). % src: docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md W90, W91 (manifests/fork/simctrl_v3.json); analysis/fork/out/bank_u_f01_20261001T134456Z.md (§3: original f01 selection frame)
Judged by the sign of the replicate mean, only 7 of the 39 flips keep their direction (95\% CI 0.075--0.335). Six reverse, and 26 vanish (15 both succeed, 2 both fail, 9 ties). Among the matched non-flip states, 11 of 39 become flips (0.15--0.45). Replicates of the same arm disagree in 41\% of flip arms and in 21--26\% of control arms. % src: analysis/fork/out/simctrl_20261001T212742Z.md
The text-difference labels did not reproduce reliably under the alternative relay setting. Simulator model, sampling and relay interface changed together, so their separate effects are not identified.

\paragraph{Sensitivity of the four-branch results.}
\label{app:simctrlpmain}
Before running, we sampled 80 Bank P states without outcome information (20 per W4 cell, stratified by domain and block) and re-ran all four arms five times each with Sonnet 5.5 as the user, one isolated single-request call per user turn (7896 calls; all integrity checks passed). % src: analysis/fork/out/simctrl_p_20261002T015200Z.md; manifests/fork/simctrl_p_v1.json
